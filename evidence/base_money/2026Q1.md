## Conflicts

None. Three RBI Weekly Statistical Supplements carry the 31 March 2026 figure: the issue of Jun 26, 2026 gives 5107831 (₹ crore) at Mar. 31 with no provisional marker (adopted); the issue of Jul 10, 2026 (the 2026Q2 extract) and the issue of Oct 23, 2026 print the same 5107831 as their Mar. 31 base column, so they do not disagree (consistency checks recorded in the [research log](../../research/base_money/2026Q1.md), not extracts). The ECB press-release headline (EUR 4,148.7 billion) agrees with the summed items (4,148,672 EUR millions) to its rounding and is a consistency check only ([extract](../../issuers/ECB/2026.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2026-03-27-published-2026-03-31)). The CBR narrow definition (19,974.5 billion rubles) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../../issuers/CBR/2026.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01042026-retrieved-2026-10-23)). The BCB SGS 1788 narrow monetary base (mar/2026, 438.897.105 c.m.u. thousand = 438897105000 BRL) is an alternative definition: it is never summed, and it is not BCB's reference value, which is SGS 20306 ([extract](../../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-mar2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22)). Printed totals that differ from the sum of their printed components by rounding are noted in their extracts and are not conflicts: FED (2,442.4 + 3,016.1 = 5,458.5 against the printed 5,458.6; the H.6 footnote says components may not add to totals due to rounding) and PBOC (components sum to 408745.67 against the printed 408745.66). The printed totals are used.
