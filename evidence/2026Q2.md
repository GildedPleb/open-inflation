## What's left out

**Banco Central do Brasil (BCB)** is not in the total. Its reserve money (series SGS 20306) is published, but we could not confirm from a BCB primary page whether it is an end-of-month or an average figure (the user's ruling of 22 Oct 2026 keeps SGS 20306 as BCB's reference series). For the coverage figure only, it is counted at BRL 1,399,128,000,000 (June 2026) × 0.19355463079454175941 USD per BRL (1 / [5.1665 BRL per USD](FX/2026.md#fx-brl-h10-weekly-release-2026-07-06-rate-of-2026-06-30)) = $270,807,703,474, from the [SGS values page](BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-jun2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22) (the [SGS JSON API](BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-jun2026-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-10-22) gives the same figure).

Coverage = $22,038,275,620,501 ÷ ($22,038,275,620,501 + $270,807,703,474) = 98.78611012590…%, shown as 98.79%. The basket's floor is 94%, so the quarter is published. (The eight issuers' individually rounded shares add up to 98.78%; the 0.01 gap is display rounding.)

**What this means for growth rates.** Because BCB is missing, this total is lower than the full basket by BCB's amount. A growth rate into or out of this quarter is only like-for-like against a quarter that also leaves BCB out. [2026Q1](2026Q1.md) does ($22,330,573,643,807); this quarter is $292,298,023,306 (1.309%) lower, a like-for-like comparison within the same basket, partly driven by exchange rates, since each currency is converted at its own quarter-end rate.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** A later RBI supplement, of 24 Jul 2026 ([original](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/WSSView.aspx?Id=28599) · [Wayback, 22 Oct 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20261022171920/https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/WSSView.aspx?Id=28599)), prints reserve money of 5,232,272 (₹ crore) on 15 July with a fortnight change of −24,093 "with respect to June 30, 2026". So 30 June = 5,232,272 + 24,093 = 5,256,365, the same as the [adopted figure](RBI/2026.md#rbi-reserve-money-2026-06-30-wss-2026-07-10): no revision. Its own figure falls in July, outside the quarter, so it is a check only and is never summed.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 3,980.3 billion ([extract](ECB/2026.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2026-06-26-published-2026-06-30)), agrees with the summed items (3,980,253 EUR millions) to its rounding.
- **Russia.** The narrow definition of the monetary base, 21,408.0 billion rubles on 1 July 2026 ([extract](CBR/2026.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01072026-retrieved-2026-10-22)), is a different definition, not a conflict. Required reserves are 495.7 in the narrow table and 588.4 in the broad file for the same date; the Bank of Russia's footnotes do not explain the difference, so it is recorded as unexplained.
- **Brazil.** The narrow monetary base, SGS 1788, June 2026: 431.100.998 (BRL thousand) = BRL 431,100,998,000 ([extract](BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22)); the JSON API gives the same value. It is an alternative definition, 0.308 of SGS 20306: never summed and not BCB's reference value. It is preliminary (marked "*" in BCB's July and September 2026 spreadsheets, Tab 28, which were not re-read for this quarter; the SGS page shows no mark).
- **Other published aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
