## What's left out

Nothing: all fourteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 June, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Certificates of Deposit / Monetary Bill), end of June, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of June, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of June, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)). Australia's figure is the RBA's own money base (notes and coins held by the private sector, banks' deposits with the RBA and other RBA liabilities to the private non-bank sector), June average (inferred), used as published (see the [RBA README](../issuers/RBA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 26 June 2020, not 30 June**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end, 26 June 2020, is used (30 June 2020 was a Tuesday). The figure comes from the supplement of 3 July 2020 and is observed, not adjusted to quarter end.

**The euro area's statement date is 26 June 2020, not 30 June**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last statement dated on or before the quarter end, as at 26 June 2020 (published 30 June), is used; the next one, as at 3 July 2020, is after the quarter end.

**Hong Kong's count date is 30 June 2020**: 30 June 2020 was a Tuesday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (Tuen Ng Festival was Thursday 25 June and HKSAR Establishment Day was Wednesday 1 July; see the [HKMA extract](../issuers/HKMA/2020.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2020-06-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-20) and [FX notes](../FX/2020.md#2020q2) for the archived 2020 gazette), and the HKMA API returns a daily figure for that date, so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** This quarter's total is $2,981,459,364,386 or 15.256% above [2020Q1](2020Q1.md)'s ($19,543,421,320,764). [2020Q3](2020Q3.md) also counts all fourteen issuers: its total ($23,860,159,914,264) is $1,335,279,229,114 or 5.928% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q2](2021Q2.md) ($28,001,092,912,974), which is $5,476,212,227,824 or 24.312% above this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** This page has no year-ago quarter, so there is no YoY count-date comparison here. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q2](2021Q2.md), whose YoY count dates are noted there.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The Federal Reserve figure was revised down from its first H.6 print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (3,163,578 ₹ crore on 26 June) is the printed total, 1 below its printed components (2,660,382 + 463,901 + 39,296 = 3,163,579, rounding). It is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2020 column (3,029,674) plus the 2020-21 "Financial Year so far" change (133,905) gives 3,163,579, 1 above 3,163,578, also from rounding ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2020.md#rbi-reserve-money-2020-06-26-wss-2020-07-03)). The next issue, dated 10 July 2020, prints a 3 July figure (3,179,786) and a week variation (16,208) that imply the same 3,163,578 on 26 June.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,193.4 billion, is the summed items (EUR 4,193,382 million) rounded to one decimal in billions ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2020.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2020-06-26-published-2020-06-30)).
- **United States.** The adopted figure (5,001.8, not seasonally adjusted monetary base, June average) is from the 23 November 2021 H.6 release, the last HTML release that still prints June 2020. The first H.6 print, in the 24 September 2020 release (when H.6 added Table 1 Monetary base following the discontinuation of H.3 on 17 September 2020), was 5,002.0 (currency 1,958.4 + reserve balances 3,043.6 = 5,002.0); it is kept as a not-adopted extract and named as superseded, under the FED latest-vintage rule. The printed total in the adopted vintage (5,001.8) is 0.1 below its printed components (1,958.3 + 3,043.6 = 5,001.9), a rounding difference in the source ([extract](../issuers/FED/2020.md#fed-h6-monetary-base-2020-06-release-2021-11-23)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (12,792.0 billion rubles on 1 July 2020) is a different definition, not a conflict. Currency issued on 1 July 2020 (12,419.5) is the same in the narrow table, which confirms the broad "June, 2020" row is the stock at the start of 1 July. The printed broad total 18,265.4 equals its printed components (12,419.5 + 3,564.7 + 656.3 + 924.7 + 700.2). The ruble rate (71.25486095 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB 10.0849 × the 30 June USD/CNY close 7.0655, per the FX rule, unrounded product) is 0.061% above the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (71.2114; denominator: the TradingView ICE close). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 71.2775 on 30 June 2020, and the cross rate is 0.032% below that close (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close) ([rate](../FX/2020.md#fx-2020q2-rub)).
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-June 2020 is 381,679,997 (R$ thousand). The SGS JSON shows no components. Cross-check, read, not extracted: Table 28 of the BCB's October 2026 monetary and credit statistics tables prints 381,680 (R$ million) for 'jun-2020', unmarked, with currency in circulation 335,060 and banking reserves 46,620 (335,060 + 46,620 = 381,680, equal to the printed total); the adopted figure rests on the archived SGS JSON ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2020.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2020-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-20)).
- **China.** The adopted figure (308,338.55 亿元) is the printed total and equals its printed components (85,413.10 + 207,202.69 + 15,722.76 = 308,338.55) ([extract](../issuers/PBOC/2020.md#pboc-reserve-money-2020-06-file-2025110510251391352)).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (408,230 AED millions, Jun 2020, August 2022 bulletin Table 4) is the printed total and equals its four printed components (106,141 + 73,287 + 50,331 + 178,471 = 408,230). Cross-check, read, not adopted: the July 2022 bulletin prints the same 408,230 and the same components for Jun 2020 ([extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2020.md#cbuae-monetary-base-jun-2020-statistical-bulletin-august-2022-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-20)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (4,530,939 NT$ millions, 2020M06, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2020.md#cbc-reserve-money-2020-06-30-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-20)). Cross-check, read, not summed: the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 45,309 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2020.06, the adopted figure rounded. The TWD rate (29.4274 per USD) is 0.114% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (29.394; denominator: the TradingView ICE close); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (220,161.7 billion won, 202006, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total and is 0.1 above its two printed components (124,305.9 + 95,855.7 = 220,161.6); the Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2020.md#bok-monetary-base-2020-06-30-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-20)); a cross-check, never summed. The KRW rate (1,199.43 per USD) is 0.040% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1,198.95; denominator: the TradingView ICE close) and 0.298% below the BOK's own Seoul close that day (1,203; denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,199.43); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and both gaps are flagged.
- **Australia.** The adopted figure (167.0 $ billion, June 2020, RBA table D3 `DMAMMB` "Money base", monthly average, inferred) is the RBA's printed total; D3 prints no components, so there is no sum to check ([extract](../issuers/RBA/2020.md#rba-d3-money-base-2020-06-retrieved-2026-11-20)). The five archived earlier D3 vintages that print June 2020 (the earliest published 26-Feb-2021) print the same figure, so no earlier print is superseded. The AUD rate (0.689891 USD per AUD, the mean of 22 weekday MarketWatch closes) is +0.013% from the TradingView ICE mean of the same weekdays (0.689804; denominator: the TradingView ICE mean).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except the Federal Reserve's adopted vintage (5,001.8, 0.1 billion dollars below its printed components), the RBI's (3,163,578, 1 crore below its printed components) and the BOK's (220,161.7, 0.1 billion won above its printed components), each noted above. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 22 weekday closes, 1 to 30 June; 1 June, Whit Monday in Switzerland, takes 29 May's close in the CHF average, and 11 June, Brazil's Corpus Christi, takes 10 June's close in the BRL average). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 June 2020 was a Tuesday; the CNY, JPY, HKD, AED, TWD, KRW and RUB rates are that day's close (RUB as the prior close before the start of 1 July), and the EUR and INR rates are Friday 26 June. GBP needed no weekday substitution. For AUD, New South Wales public holidays in the month average: 8 June (Queen's Birthday) takes 5 June's close (0.690136 without the substitution). The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2020.md#2020q2). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check are (MarketWatch − TradingView) / TradingView ICE close, flagged here and in each [FX row](../FX/2020.md#2020q2) with its denominator, since there is no divergence limit: TWD +0.114%; RUB +0.061%; KRW +0.040%; EUR +0.030%; CHF +0.024%; INR +0.016%; GBP +0.015%; AUD +0.013%; AED +0.011%; HKD +0.008%; CNY +0.007%; BRL +0.007%; JPY −0.014%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $22,523,559,651,719, 0.006% below the adopted total. The BOK's official Seoul close on 30 June (1,203) is 0.298% above the adopted MarketWatch close (denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,199.43), also flagged. The adopted ruble cross is 0.032% below the Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB close that day (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close 71.2775).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
