## What's left out

Nothing: all thirteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 March, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of March, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of March, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of March, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 31 March 2022, the quarter-end date itself**: the RBI publishes a final reserve-money figure for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) that date is used, even though 31 March 2022 was a Thursday rather than a Friday. The figure comes from the supplement of 1 July 2022, issued after the RBI balance sheet (by end-May), so it is final.

**The euro area's statement date is 25 March 2022, not 31 March**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last statement dated on or before the quarter end, as at 25 March 2022 (published 29 March), is used; the next one, as at 1 April 2022, is after the quarter end.

**Hong Kong's count date is 31 March 2022**: 31 March 2022 was a Thursday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (Ching Ming Festival and Good Friday fell in April), so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** [2022Q2](2022Q2.md) also counts all thirteen issuers: its total ($25,890,228,341,322) is $1,809,863,060,209 or 6.534% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2023Q1](2023Q1.md) ($25,597,583,145,888), which is $2,102,508,255,643 or 7.590% below this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** This page has no year-ago quarter, so there is no YoY count-date comparison here. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2023Q1](2023Q1.md), whose YoY count dates are noted there.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The UAE figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,068,887 ₹ crore on 31 March) is the printed total; its printed components add up to 4,068,886, 1 below it, a rounding difference in the published components. It is consistent within the same supplement: the 24 June 2022 column (4,103,867) less the printed 2022-23 "Financial Year so far" change (34,980) gives 4,068,887. A later supplement implies a slightly higher 24 June 2022 figure but the same 31 March 2022 level: the 30 June 2023 supplement implies 4,104,025 for 24 June 2022 and prints a 2022-23 financial-year-so-far variation of 35,138, and 4,104,025 less 35,138 equals the adopted 4,068,887. No supplement found prints a revised 31 March 2022 level ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2022.md#rbi-reserve-money-2022-03-31-wss-2022-07-01)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 6,174.5 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 6,174,505 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2022.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2022-03-25-published-2022-03-29)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (14,947.0 billion rubles on 1 April 2022) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2022.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01042022-retrieved-2026-11-10)); its printed total equals its printed components (14,842.3 + 104.7). The two tables differ on required reserves (104.7 narrow vs 150 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 April 2022 (14,842.3) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Mar, 2022" row is the stock at the start of 1 April. The day before the start of 1 April, 31 March, was a Thursday with Moscow Exchange trading, so the rate is that day's close (`prior close`). The ruble rate (85.016864 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is **4.011% above** the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (81.7387), the largest FX gap this quarter. Both legs are from the same day (the USD/CNY leg is the CNY row's 31 March close, 6.3400). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 83.2 on 31 March 2022, 1.79% above TradingView, and the cross rate is 2.18% above that close ([rate](../FX/2022.md#fx-2022q1-rub)). Converted at the TradingView close instead, Russia's figure would be $234,730,916,934 rather than $225,679,930,984. The product's standing rule is the CNY/RUB MOEX cross, so the MarketWatch-leg cross is used and the gap is flagged.
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-March 2022 is 385,897,343 (R$ thousand). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 805,133,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2022.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-mar2022-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-10)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 2,147,536 (HK$ million) for 2022-03 as the adopted daily figure for 2022-03-31, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2022.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2022-03-31-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-10)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (478,895 AED million, Mar 2022, Statistical Bulletin May 2024, the latest bulletin still served by the CBUAE that prints March 2022) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. The April 2022 bulletin, the earliest evidenced print, printed 480,995, unmarked, with the same Currency Issued, Reserve Requirements and Monetary Bills but current accounts and overnight deposits of 115,758 rather than 113,658. Later bulletins through August 2023 still printed 480,995. The September 2023 bulletin is the earliest that prints the revised 478,895 (current accounts and overnight deposits 113,658), and it footnotes that row "Revised to account for amendments in Overnight Deposit Facility data"; later bulletins through May 2024 keep 478,895 unmarked. The later, unmarked figure is used under the [CBUAE vintage rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions). The revision is AED 2,100 million (0.44%), all in that line. The April 2022, August 2023 and September 2023 bulletins are read cross-checks, never summed (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2022.md#cbuae-monetary-base-mar-2022-statistical-bulletin-may-2024-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-10)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (5,547,958 NT$ millions, 2022M03, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals; the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 55480 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2022M03, the same figure rounded ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2022.md#cbc-reserve-money-2022-03-31-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-10)). The TWD rate (28.6442 per USD) is **0.102% above** the TradingView ICE close for the same day (28.615), the second-largest FX gap this quarter; the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (265,200.8 billion won, 202203, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total and equals its two printed components; the Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2022.md#bok-monetary-base-2022-03-31-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-11)); a cross-check, never summed. The KRW rate (1214.94 per USD) is 0.063% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1214.17); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except China's (components 0.01 亿元 below the printed total) and India's (components 1 crore below the printed total, noted above). The CBR's broad table is 0.1 below its components (14,842.3 + 1,086.4 + 150 + 3,107.8 = 19,186.5 against a printed 19,186.6), a rounding difference, and the printed total is used. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, equals its components.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 23 weekday closes, 1 to 31 March). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The quarter-end date 31 March 2022 was a Thursday, so every single-day rate dated on it is that day's close; the EUR rate is the 25 March close, the date of the ECB statement. 1 March was Carnival in Brazil, so under the holiday rule it takes 25 February's close in the BRL month average (4.970874; 4.970757 without the substitution). There was no March bank holiday in the UK and no March holiday for CHF, so no GBP or CHF close is substituted. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2022.md#2022q1). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check, flagged here since there is no divergence limit: RUB +4.011%; TWD 28.6442 vs 28.615, +0.102%; KRW 1214.94 vs 1214.17, +0.063%; CHF 0.929452 vs 0.928992, +0.050%; JPY 121.70 vs 121.66, +0.033%; CNY 6.3400 vs 6.342, −0.032%; GBP 1.316600 vs 1.316348, +0.019%; BRL 4.970874 vs 4.970052, +0.017%; INR 75.92 vs 75.931, −0.014%; AED 3.6730 vs 3.6726, +0.011%; HKD 7.8327 vs 7.83199, +0.009%; EUR 1.0982 vs 1.0982, 0.000%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $27,709,778,814,587, 0.035% above the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
