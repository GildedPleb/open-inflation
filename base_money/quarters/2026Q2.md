## What's left out

Nothing: all eleven basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 June, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of June, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**Growth rates.** [2026Q1](2026Q1.md) also counts all eleven issuers ($22,952,873,136,892); this quarter is $349,430,560,524 (1.522%) lower, partly driven by exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** A later RBI supplement, of 24 Jul 2026, prints reserve money of 5,232,272 (₹ crore) on 15 July with a fortnight change of −24,093 "with respect to June 30, 2026". So 30 June = 5,232,272 + 24,093 = 5,256,365, the same as the [adopted figure](../issuers/RBI/2026.md#rbi-reserve-money-2026-06-30-wss-2026-07-10): no revision. Its own figure falls in July, outside the quarter, so it is a check only and is never summed.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 3,980.3 billion ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2026.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2026-06-26-published-2026-06-30)), agrees with the summed items (3,980,253 EUR millions) to its rounding.
- **Russia.** The narrow definition of the monetary base, 21,408.0 billion rubles on 1 July 2026 ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2026.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01072026-retrieved-2026-10-26)), is a different definition, not a conflict. Required reserves are 495.7 in the narrow table and 588.4 in the broad file for the same date; the Bank of Russia's footnotes do not explain the difference, so it is recorded as unexplained.
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 431,101 (R$ million) for 'jun-2026*', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22)); its '*' marks June 2026 as preliminary, so the figure is provisional. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,399,128,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-jun2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-26)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 2,075,020 (HK$ million) for 2026-06 as the adopted daily figure for 2026-06-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2026.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2026-06-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-28)); a cross-check, never summed. The HKD rate (7.8415 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (7.8415).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (783,545 AED million, Jun 2026, Statistical Bulletin August 2026) is the printed total and equals its four printed components; the Statistical Bulletin June 2026 prints the same 783,545 for Jun 2026 (there its latest, preliminary month), and the CBUAE's Monetary & Banking Developments summary for June 2026 says "The monetary base stood at AED 783.5 billion, at the end of June 2026." (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2026.md#cbuae-monetary-base-jun-2026-statistical-bulletin-august-2026-table-3-retrieved-2026-10-29)); a cross-check, never summed. The AED rate (3.6727 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (3.6727).
- **Other published aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
