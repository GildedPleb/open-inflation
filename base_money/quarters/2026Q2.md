## What's left out

Nothing: all nine basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**Growth rates.** [2026Q1](2026Q1.md) also counts all nine issuers ($22,449,782,815,379); this quarter is $324,303,573,123 (1.445%) lower, partly driven by exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** A later RBI supplement, of 24 Jul 2026, prints reserve money of 5,232,272 (₹ crore) on 15 July with a fortnight change of −24,093 "with respect to June 30, 2026". So 30 June = 5,232,272 + 24,093 = 5,256,365, the same as the [adopted figure](../issuers/RBI/2026.md#rbi-reserve-money-2026-06-30-wss-2026-07-10): no revision. Its own figure falls in July, outside the quarter, so it is a check only and is never summed.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 3,980.3 billion ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2026.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2026-06-26-published-2026-06-30)), agrees with the summed items (3,980,253 EUR millions) to its rounding.
- **Russia.** The narrow definition of the monetary base, 21,408.0 billion rubles on 1 July 2026 ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2026.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01072026-retrieved-2026-10-22)), is a different definition, not a conflict. Required reserves are 495.7 in the narrow table and 588.4 in the broad file for the same date; the Bank of Russia's footnotes do not explain the difference, so it is recorded as unexplained.
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 431,101 (R$ million) for 'jun-2026*', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted, because no archive could capture the workbook; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22)); its '*' marks June 2026 as preliminary, so the figure is provisional. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,399,128,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-jun2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22)).
- **Other published aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
