## What's left out

Nothing: all ten basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 28 June, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 28 June 2024, not 30 June**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before quarter end is used.

**Hong Kong's count date is 28 June 2024, not 30 June**: 30 June 2024 was a Sunday and the HKMA publishes no daily figure for it (the API returns "No result found"), so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last business day before it is used.

**Growth rates.** [2024Q3](2024Q3.md) also counts all ten issuers: its total ($23,860,297,291,603) is $808,460,149,068 or 3.507% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $722,072,290,412 (3.037%) lower than [2024Q1](2024Q1.md)'s ($23,773,909,432,947).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,694,929 ₹ crore on 28 June) is the printed total and equals its printed components. The next weekly issue (12 July 2024) implies 4,694,929 from its printed week change, the same figure, so no revision is seen ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2024.md#rbi-reserve-money-2024-06-28-wss-2024-07-05)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,797.4 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,797,400 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2024.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2024-06-28-published-2024-07-03)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,145.8 billion rubles on 1 July 2024) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2024.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01072024-retrieved-2026-10-29)). The two tables differ on required reserves (382.8 narrow vs 504.1 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 July (17,763.0) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Jun, 2024" row is the stock at the start of 1 July (likewise 17,760.9 on 1 June for "May, 2024"). The day before the start of 1 July, 30 June, was a Sunday with no Moscow Exchange trading (no rows for 29 or 30 June), so the rate is the last close before it, Friday 28 June. The ruble rate (85.27813185 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is 0.55% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (85.7495), the largest FX gap this quarter; TradingView's USD/RUB is an over-the-counter quote, since the Moscow Exchange suspended USD trading from 13 June 2024 (announced 12 June); the archived Moscow Exchange notice and Bank of Russia press release are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2024.md#2024q2) ([rate](../FX/2024.md#fx-2024q2-rub)).
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 436,902 (R$ million) for 'jun-2024', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-29)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,264,303,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-jun2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-29)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,926,927 (HK$ million) for 2024-06 as the adopted daily figure for 2024-06-28, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2024.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2024-06-28-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-29)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The Fed's components for June 2024 (currency 2,352.2 + reserves 3,379.5) sum to 5,731.7 billion dollars, 0.1 below the printed monetary base 5,731.8 ("Components may not add to totals due to rounding"); the printed total is used. Every other printed total equals its components (the PBOC's and the CBR's exactly this quarter); the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 20 weekday closes, 3 to 28 June). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 June 2024 was a Sunday, so the CNY, JPY and RUB rates are the last close before it, Friday 28 June. The largest gap to the TradingView ICE cross-check is RUB (above); among the other rates it is EUR (1.0716 vs 1.07112, 0.04%); converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $23,049,795,795,835, 0.009% below the adopted total. No rate date or averaged weekday was a local-market holiday ([FX](../FX/2024.md#2024q2)).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
