## What's left out

Nothing: all eleven basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 March, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of March, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 31 March 2025, the quarter-end date itself**: the RBI publishes a final reserve-money figure for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) that date is used rather than the last Friday. The figure comes from the supplement of 4 July 2025, issued after the RBI balance sheet (by end-May), so it is final.

**Growth rates.** [2025Q2](2025Q2.md) also counts all eleven issuers: its total ($23,922,514,650,921) is $618,301,543,215 or 2.653% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,829,243 ₹ crore on 31 March) is the base of the 2025-26 "Financial Year so far" change printed in the same supplement: 4,923,705 on 27 June less the printed change 94,463 gives 4,829,242, 1 off, a rounding difference in the printed change ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-03-31-wss-2025-07-04)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,549 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,548,983 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2025.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2025-03-28-published-2025-04-01)).
- **Japan.** The BOJ's March 2025 monthly release (base2503.pdf, dated 2 April 2025) prints the same end-of-period figures as the adopted long-series spreadsheet; the BOJ site no longer serves that PDF, so it is cited from existing Wayback captures as a cross-check in the [extract](../issuers/BOJ/2025.md#boj-monetary-base-end-of-period-2025-03-mblongxlsx-retrieved-2026-10-26).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (17,893.8 billion rubles on 1 April 2025) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2025.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01042025-retrieved-2026-10-27)). The two tables differ on required reserves (457.1 narrow vs 560.8 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 April (17,436.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Mar, 2025" row is the stock at the start of 1 April (likewise 17,693.3 on 1 March for "Feb, 2025"). The ruble rate (84.7597 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is 2.1% above the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (82.9956); the FX rule's Moscow Exchange route is used as written ([FX](../FX/2025.md#fx-2025q1-rub)).
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 443,277 (R$ million) for 'mar-2025', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-mar2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-27)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,316,901,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-mar2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-27)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,977,117 (HK$ million) for 2025-03 as the adopted daily figure for 2025-03-31, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2025.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2025-03-31-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-28)); a cross-check, never summed. The HKD rate (7.7797 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (7.7797).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (833,102 AED million, Mar 2025, Statistical Bulletin February 2026) is the printed total and equals its four printed components; the CBUAE's "UAE Monetary Base (Monthly)" release for February 2026 prints 833.1 (AED billion) for Mar-25, the adopted 833,102 (AED million) rounded (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2025.md#cbuae-monetary-base-mar-2025-statistical-bulletin-february-2026-table-4-retrieved-2026-10-29)); a cross-check, never summed. The AED rate (3.6729 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (3.6729). On 31 March 2025 the last USD/AED bar at or before 22:00 UTC is at 19:00 UTC, which is the close used.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The CBR's broad-base components for March 2025 sum to 26,937.4 billion rubles, 0.1 above the printed total 26,937.3; the printed total is used. Every other printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
