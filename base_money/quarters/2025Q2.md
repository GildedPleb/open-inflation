## What's left out

Nothing: all nine basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 27 June 2025, not 30 June**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before quarter end is used.

**Growth rates.** [2025Q3](2025Q3.md) also counts all nine issuers: its total ($22,882,534,748,618) is $535,711,499,311 or 2.288% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,923,705 ₹ crore on 27 June) is consistent with the next supplement, 11 Jul 2025 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-06-27-wss-2025-07-04)), which prints 4,922,861 for 4 July and a week change of -844, implying the same figure for 27 June.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,430.1 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,430,135 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2025.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2025-06-27-published-2025-07-01)).
- **Japan.** The BOJ's June 2025 monthly release (base2506.pdf, dated 2 July 2025) prints the same end-of-period figures as the adopted long-series spreadsheet; the BOJ site no longer serves that PDF, so it is cited from two existing Wayback captures as a cross-check in the [extract](../issuers/BOJ/2025.md#boj-monetary-base-end-of-period-2025-06-mblongxlsx-retrieved-2026-10-26).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,242.5 billion rubles on 1 July 2025) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2025.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01072025-retrieved-2026-10-26)). The two tables differ on required reserves (452.8 narrow vs 554.8 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 July (17,789.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Jun, 2025" row is the stock at the start of 1 July (likewise 17,575.0 on 1 June for "May, 2025").
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 451,010 (R$ million) for 'jun-2025', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-26)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,308,055,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-jun2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-26)).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The CBR's broad-base components for June 2025 sum to 26,659.5 billion rubles, 0.1 above the printed total 26,659.4; the printed total is used. Every other printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
