## What's left out

Nothing: all eleven basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 March, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of March, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 31 March 2023, the quarter-end date itself**: the RBI publishes a final reserve-money figure for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) that date is used; in 2023 it was also the last Friday of the quarter. The figure comes from the supplement of 7 July 2023, issued after the RBI balance sheet (by end-May), so it is final.

**Growth rates.** [2023Q2](2023Q2.md) also counts all eleven issuers: its total ($24,251,108,419,129) is $-942,875,115,666 or 3.742% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source used now prints a figure that disagrees with an adopted one. The UAE figure was revised down from its first, preliminary print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,386,759 ₹ crore on 31 March) is the printed total and equals its printed components (3,378,521 + 930,477 + 77,761). It is consistent within the same supplement: the 30 June 2023 column (4,370,091) less the printed 2023-24 "Financial Year so far" change (−16,668) gives 4,386,759 ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2023.md#rbi-reserve-money-2023-03-31-wss-2023-07-07)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,590.5 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 5,590,515 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2023.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2023-03-31-published-2023-04-05)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (16,855.8 billion rubles on 1 April 2023) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2023.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01042023-retrieved-2026-11-05)). The two tables differ on required reserves (194.8 narrow vs 265.4 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 April 2023 (16,661.0) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Mar, 2023" row is the stock at the start of 1 April (likewise 16,421.5 on 1 March for "Feb, 2023"). The day before the start of 1 April, 31 March, was a Friday and a Moscow Exchange trading day (no rows for 1 and 2 April, a weekend), so the rate is the 31 March close. The ruble rate (77.4869328 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is **0.63% below** the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (77.976), the largest FX gap this quarter. Both legs are from the same day (the USD/CNY leg is the CNY row's 31 March close, 6.8688). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 77.5975 on 31 March 2023, 0.49% below TradingView, and the cross rate is 0.14% below that close ([rate](../FX/2023.md#fx-2023q1-rub)). Converted at the TradingView close instead, Russia's figure would be $305,077,203,242 rather than $307,002,731,175.
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 413,314 (R$ million) for 'mar-2023', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded, with currency in circulation 330,756 and bank reserves 82,558 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-mar2023-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-05)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. The SGS values page answered, so it is the adopted source, and the SGS JSON API response for the same series, archived, prints the same figure (see the extract). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 975,522,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-mar2023-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-05)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,903,563 (HK$ million) for 2023-03 as the adopted daily figure for 2023-03-31, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2023.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2023-03-31-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-05)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (555,755 AED million, Mar 2023, Statistical Bulletin May 2025, the latest bulletin still served by the CBUAE that prints March 2023; from the June 2025 bulletin on, the table no longer prints March 2023) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. The March 2023 bulletin first printed 568,855, marked "* Preliminary", with the same Currency Issued, Reserve Requirements and Monetary Bills but current accounts and overnight deposits of 147,975 rather than 134,875. The figure was revised down by AED 13,100 million (2.3%), all in that line, between the first print and the May 2025 bulletin; the CBUAE gives no reason, so the revision is recorded as unexplained, and the later, unmarked figure is used under the [CBUAE vintage rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions). The March 2023 bulletin is a read cross-check, never summed (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2023.md#cbuae-monetary-base-mar-2023-statistical-bulletin-may-2025-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-05)).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The printed totals are used, and every one equals its printed components: the Fed's (2,312.6 + 3,258.4 = 5,571.0 billion dollars), the PBOC's, the BoJ's, the SNB's, the RBI's, the CBR broad table's (16,661.0 + 3,317.0 + 265.4 + 3,545.3 = 23,788.7 billion rubles), the HKMA's and the CBUAE's. The BCB's SGS page shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, also equals its components (16,661.0 + 194.8 = 16,855.8).
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 23 weekday closes, 1 to 31 March). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 March 2023 was a Friday, so the CNY, EUR, JPY, INR, HKD and AED rates are the 31 March close, and so is the RUB rate (the last close before the start of 1 April). There was no March holiday in Brazil, the UK or Switzerland (Brazil's Carnival fell on 21 February and Good Friday on 7 April), so no BRL, GBP or CHF close is substituted in the month averages; Eid Al-Fitr fell in April, so the AED rate needs no holiday substitution. The holiday calendars are cited, archived, in the [FX notes](../FX/2023.md#2023q1). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check, flagged here since there is no divergence limit: RUB −0.63% (above); INR 82.18 vs 82.14, +0.049%; EUR 1.0843 vs 1.08381, +0.045%; CHF 0.925665 vs 0.925260, +0.044%; CNY 6.8688 vs 6.8671, +0.025%; GBP 1.214378 vs 1.214139, +0.020%; AED 3.6727 vs 3.6724, +0.008%; BRL 5.203022 vs 5.202761, +0.005%; HKD 7.8491 vs 7.8493, −0.003%; JPY 132.76 vs 132.797, −0.028%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $25,189,523,703,597, 0.018% below the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
