## What's left out

Nothing: all thirteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 March, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Certificates of Deposit / Monetary Bill), end of March, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of March, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of March, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 31 March 2021**: the RBI publishes a final reserve-money figure for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) that date is used, even though 31 March 2021 was a Wednesday rather than a Friday. The figure comes from the supplement of 2 July 2021, issued after the RBI balance sheet (by end-May), and is observed and final, not interpolated or adjusted.

**The euro area's statement date is 26 March 2021, not 31 March**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last statement dated on or before the quarter end, as at 26 March 2021 (published 30 March), is used; the next one is after the quarter end.

**Hong Kong's count date is 31 March 2021**: 31 March 2021 was a Wednesday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (Good Friday was 2 April), and the HKMA API returns a daily figure for that date, so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** This quarter's total is $7,314,923,223,704 or 37.593% above [2020Q1](2020Q1.md)'s ($19,458,183,351,164). It is $909,676,982,143 or 3.517% above [2020Q4](2020Q4.md)'s ($25,863,429,592,725); part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. [2021Q2](2021Q2.md) also counts all thirteen issuers: its total ($27,702,425,607,974) is $929,319,033,106 or 3.471% above this quarter's. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2022Q1](2022Q1.md) ($27,700,091,401,531), which is $926,984,826,663 or 3.462% above this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** India's YoY compares 31 March 2021 with 31 March 2020 (both fiscal year-ends), the ECB's compares the week to 26 March 2021 with the week to 27 March 2020, Hong Kong's compares 31 March 2021 with 31 March 2020, and Korea's compares the end of March 2021 with the end of March 2020, both end-of-month stocks; each follows its issuer's count-date rule. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2022Q1](2022Q1.md), whose YoY count dates are noted there.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The Federal Reserve figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (3,599,981 ₹ crore on 31 March) is the printed total and equals its printed components. It comes from the supplement dated 2 July 2021, after the RBI balance sheet, so it is final. Consistency check against a later issue, read, not adopted: the supplement dated 1 October 2021 also prints the Mar. 31 column as 3,599,981, equal to the adopted figure ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2021.md#rbi-reserve-money-2021-03-31-wss-2021-07-02)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,533 billion, is the summed items (EUR 5,533,008 million) rounded to the nearest billion ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2021.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2021-03-26-published-2021-03-30)).
- **United States.** The adopted figure (5,839.0, not seasonally adjusted monetary base, March average) is from the 23 August 2022 H.6 release, the last HTML release that still prints March 2021. The first print, in the 27 April 2021 release, was 5,839.1 (currency 2,117.8 + reserve balances 3,721.3 = 5,839.1); it is kept as a not-adopted extract and named as superseded, under the FED latest-vintage rule. The printed total in the adopted vintage (5,839.0) is 0.1 below its printed components (2,117.8 + 3,721.3 = 5,839.1) ([extract](../issuers/FED/2021.md#fed-h6-monetary-base-2021-03-release-2022-08-23)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (13,732.3 billion rubles on 1 April 2021) is a different definition, not a conflict. Currency issued on 1 April 2021 (13,314.3) is the same in the narrow table, which confirms the broad "Mar, 2021" row is the stock at the start of 1 April. The printed broad total 19,415.9 is 0.1 below its printed components (13,314.3 + 2,947.9 + 740.9 + 1,772.1 + 640.8 = 19,416.0), a rounding difference, and the printed total is used. The ruble rate (75.618158 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB 11.54 × the 31 March USD/CNY close 6.5527, per the FX rule, unrounded product) is 0.047% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (75.654; denominator: the TradingView ICE close). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 75.69 on 31 March 2021, and the cross rate is 0.095% below that close (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close) ([rate](../FX/2021.md#fx-2021q1-rub)).
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-March 2021 is 405,630,283 (R$ thousand). The SGS JSON shows no components. Cross-check, read, not extracted: Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 monetary and credit statistics tables prints 405,630 (R$ million) for 'mar-2021', unmarked, with currency in circulation 343,091 and bank reserves 62,540 (343,091 + 62,540 = 405,631, 1 above the printed 405,630, rounding); the adopted figure rests on the archived SGS JSON ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2021.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-mar2021-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-19)).
- **China.** The adopted figure (326,956.16 亿元) is the printed total and equals its printed components ([extract](../issuers/PBOC/2021.md#pboc-reserve-money-2021-03-file-2025110510260596858)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (5,061,479 NT$ millions, 2021M03, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2021.md#cbc-reserve-money-2021-03-31-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)). Cross-check, read, not summed: the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 50,615 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2021.03, the adopted figure rounded. The TWD rate (28.4593 per USD) is 0.219% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (28.397; denominator: the TradingView ICE close); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (234,792.4 billion won, 202103, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total and equals its two printed components; the Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2021.md#bok-monetary-base-2021-03-31-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)); a cross-check, never summed. The KRW rate (1,127.97 per USD) is 0.052% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1,127.38; denominator: the TradingView ICE close) and 0.340% below the BOK's own Seoul close that day (1,131.8; denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,127.97); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and both gaps are flagged.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except the Federal Reserve's (0.1 billion dollars in the adopted vintage; the first print equals its components), the CBR's broad total (0.1 billion rubles below its components) and Table 28's 405,630 (R$ million), 1 below its printed components, each noted above. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 23 weekday closes, 1 to 31 March; no weekday substitution). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 March 2021 was a Wednesday; the CNY, JPY, HKD, AED, TWD, KRW and INR rates are that day's close, the RUB rate is the previous day's close (`prior close`) before the start of 1 April, and the EUR rate is Friday 26 March. GBP, CHF and BRL needed no weekday substitution. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2021.md#2021q1). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check are (MarketWatch − TradingView) / TradingView ICE close, flagged here and in each [FX row](../FX/2021.md#2021q1) with its denominator, since there is no divergence limit: TWD +0.219%; INR +0.086%; KRW +0.052%; RUB −0.047%; EUR +0.027%; CHF +0.023%; GBP +0.012%; JPY +0.010%; BRL +0.010%; AED +0.008%; HKD +0.005%; CNY +0.002%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $26,772,851,275,402, 0.001% below the adopted total. The BOK's official Seoul close on 31 March (1,131.8) is 0.340% above the adopted MarketWatch close (denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,127.97), also flagged. The adopted ruble cross is 0.095% below the Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB close that day (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close 75.69).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
