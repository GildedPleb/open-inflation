## What's left out

**Banco Central do Brasil (BCB)** is not in the total. Its reserve money (series SGS 20306) is published, but we could not confirm from a BCB primary page whether it is an end-of-month or an average figure. For the coverage figure only, it is counted at BRL 1,376,580,000,000 (December 2025) × 0.18309783594836714265 USD per BRL (1 / [5.461561 BRL per USD](../FX/2025.md#fx-2025q4-brl)) = $252,048,819,010 from the [SGS values page](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-dec2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23) (the [SGS JSON API](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-dec2025-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-10-23) gives the same figure).

Coverage = $22,472,588,647,198 ÷ ($22,472,588,647,198 + $252,048,819,010) = 98.89085658952…%, shown as 98.89%. The basket's floor is 94%, so the quarter is published. (The eight issuers' individually rounded shares add up to 98.90%; the 0.01 gap is display rounding.)

**What this means for growth rates.** Because BCB is missing, this total is lower than the full basket by BCB's amount. A growth rate into or out of this quarter is only like-for-like against a quarter that also leaves BCB out. [2026Q1](2026Q1.md) does ($22,365,865,359,611, $106,723,287,587 or 0.475% lower than 2025Q4), so that comparison is fair within the same basket; part of it is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** A later final RBI supplement, 30 Jan 2026 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-12-31-wss-2026-01-09)), prints 4,862,647 for 15 January and a fortnight change of 63,512 "With respect to December 31, 2025", which implies the same 4,799,135 for 31 December, so no revision is seen. The 31 December source issue (9 Jan 2026) carries no provisional marker; its "*" on Dec. 31 is a footnote on the new 15th-and-month-end publication dates under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,243.3 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,243,284 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2025.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2025-12-26-published-2025-12-30)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (19,901.9 billion rubles on 1 January 2026) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2025.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01012026-retrieved-2026-10-23)). The two tables differ on required reserves (452.2 narrow vs 554.1 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in 2026Q1 and 2026Q2. Currency issued on 1 January (19,449.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Dec, 2025" row is the stock at the start of 1 January (likewise 18,613.4 on 1 December for "Nov, 2025").
- **Brazil.** BCB's narrow monetary base (SGS 1788, BRL 447,096,654,000 for December) is an alternative definition, never summed and not the reference value ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-dec2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23)); it is 0.325 of SGS 20306.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The RBI's three components add to 4,799,136 against the printed 4,799,135. We use the printed total. Every other printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
