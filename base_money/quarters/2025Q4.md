## What's left out

Nothing: all ten basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 December, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**Growth rates.** Every quarter counts all ten issuers, so comparisons are like-for-like. [2026Q1](2026Q1.md) is $22,713,241,218,349, $103,369,457,731 or 0.453% lower than 2025Q4; part of it is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $325,528,030,389 (1.407%) lower than [2025Q3](2025Q3.md)'s ($23,142,138,706,469).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** A later final RBI supplement, 30 Jan 2026 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-12-31-wss-2026-01-09)), prints 4,862,647 for 15 January and a fortnight change of 63,512 "With respect to December 31, 2025", which implies the same 4,799,135 for 31 December, so no revision is seen. The 31 December source issue (9 Jan 2026) carries no provisional marker; its "*" on Dec. 31 is a footnote on the new 15th-and-month-end publication dates under the Banking Laws (Amendment) Act, 2025.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,243.3 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,243,284 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2025.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2025-12-26-published-2025-12-30)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (19,901.9 billion rubles on 1 January 2026) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2025.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01012026-retrieved-2026-10-26)). The two tables differ on required reserves (452.2 narrow vs 554.1 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in 2026Q1 and 2026Q2. Currency issued on 1 January (19,449.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Dec, 2025" row is the stock at the start of 1 January (likewise 18,613.4 on 1 December for "Nov, 2025").
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 447,097 (R$ million) for 'dez-2025', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-dec2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,376,580,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-dec2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-26)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 2,040,126 (HK$ million) for 2025-12 as the adopted daily figure for 2025-12-31, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2025.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2025-12-31-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-28)); a cross-check, never summed. The HKD rate (7.782 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (7.782).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The RBI's three components add to 4,799,136 against the printed 4,799,135. We use the printed total. BCB's Table 28 components (368,625 + 78,471 = 447,096) differ from its printed 447,097 by rounding to R$ million; the adopted SGS 1788 figure prints no components. Every other printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
