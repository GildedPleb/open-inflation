## What's left out

- **Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE): not yet published.** The CBUAE first prints a month's monetary base in its monthly Statistical Bulletin, and the September 2026 bulletin is not out as of 5 November 2026: the latest is the August 2026 bulletin, dated 6 October 2026 (the June and July bulletins were dated 28 July and 9 September 2026). Its figure is never estimated or carried forward. Its reference value, the latest figure inside the quarter, is the August 2026 monetary base, AED 825,885,000,000 (provisional), × [3.6728 AED per USD](../FX/2026.md#fx-2026q3-aed) = $224,865,225,441 ([reference-only extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2026.md#cbuae-monetary-base-aug-2026-statistical-bulletin-august-2026-table-3-reference-only-retrieved-2026-11-05)); it is a coverage weight only and is never summed. When the September bulletin is published, the CBUAE is added to this quarter as a revision.

Coverage: $22,639,266,952,424 ÷ ($22,639,266,952,424 + $224,865,225,441) = $22,639,266,952,424 ÷ $22,864,132,177,865 = 99.02%, above the 94% floor (see [Coverage](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#coverage)).

This total leaves out CBUAE. The CSV value is lower by the amount of the missing issuers, so growth rates into and out of this quarter are distorted and should not be read as changes in base money.

Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 September, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of September, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**Growth rates.** [2026Q2](2026Q2.md) counts all twelve issuers ($22,834,824,662,358), so the two totals are not comparable: this quarter is $195,557,709,934 lower, and most of that gap is the missing CBUAE. Without the CBUAE, 2026Q2 comes to $22,621,481,617,731, and this quarter is $17,785,334,693 higher than that. That change is also partly driven by exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. The Total has no YoY for the same reason: [2025Q3](2025Q3.md) counts all twelve issuers.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** A later RBI supplement, of 23 Oct 2026, prints reserve money of 5,324,881 (₹ crore) on 15 October with a fortnight change of 46,739 "With respect to September 30, 2026". So 30 September = 5,324,881 − 46,739 = 5,278,142, the same as the [adopted figure](../issuers/RBI/2026.md#rbi-reserve-money-2026-09-30-wss-2026-10-09): no revision. Its own figure falls in October, outside the quarter, so it is a check only and is never summed.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 3,925.6 billion ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2026.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2026-09-25-published-2026-09-29)), agrees with the summed items (3,925,631 EUR millions) to its rounding.
- **Russia.** The narrow definition of the monetary base, 22,730.6 billion rubles on 1 October 2026 ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2026.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102026-retrieved-2026-11-05)), is a different definition, not a conflict. Required reserves are 496.1 in the narrow table and 587.6 in the broad file for the same date; the Bank of Russia's footnotes do not explain the difference, so it is recorded as unexplained. **FX divergence:** the RUB rate (82.8763164 RUB per USD, the Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB close × USD/CNY) is −0.60% from the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close (83.3774); at the ICE close the CBR figure would be $395,523,247,307, $2,391,397,461 less. The adopted rate follows the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule); the gap is flagged, not adjusted. Every other rate is within 0.08% of its TradingView ICE close.
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's October 2026 statistics tables prints 434,923 (R$ million) for 'set-2026*', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded, with currency in circulation 382,359 and bank reserves 52,563, which sum to 434,922 (rounding; read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-05)); its '*' marks September 2026 as preliminary, so the figure is provisional. The same page and table still print June 2026 unchanged and still preliminary, so 2026Q2 is not revised. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,459,651,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-05)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 2,090,204 (HK$ million) for 2026-09 as the adopted daily figure for 2026-09-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2026.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2026-09-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-05)); a cross-check, never summed. The HKD rate (7.8453 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (7.84522).
- **UAE.** Excluded (see What's left out). The reference value's August figure is the bulletin's latest, preliminary month; its four printed components sum to 825,884, 1 below the printed 825,885 (rounding). The Jun 2026 figure counted for 2026Q2 (783,545) is unchanged, since no bulletin newer than August 2026 exists. The AED rate (3.6728 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (3.6728).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (7,504,266 NT$ millions, 2026M09, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals; the CBC's ERM-H End of Period Amount prints 75043 (NT$ hundred millions), the adopted figure rounded (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBC/2026.md#cbc-reserve-money-2026-09-30-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-05)); a cross-check, never summed. The TWD rate (31.874 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (31.874).
- **Other published aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
