ᵖ Uses a provisional bank figure, from this quarter or a year earlier, that may be revised.

Each USD figure is the local amount × the FX rate, rounded to the nearest dollar (half-to-even); the total is their sum. Click an issuer for the bank's basic facts, a USD figure for the exact quote it comes from, and a rate for the exchange-rate source (each issuer extract carries the original and archived links). FX rates are market closes on or just before each report's count date (each FX row says which), or the mean of that month's weekday closes for a monthly figure (BOE, SNB, BCB, RBA); see [FX](../FX/2026.md#2026q3).

## What's left out

Nothing: all fourteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 September, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of September, the bulletin's latest, preliminary month, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of September, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of September, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)). Australia's figure is the RBA's own money base (notes and coins held by the private sector, banks' deposits with the RBA and other RBA liabilities to the private non-bank sector), September average (inferred), used as published (see the [RBA README](../issuers/RBA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**Growth rates.** [2026Q2](2026Q2.md) also counts all fourteen issuers ($23,233,925,859,683); this quarter is $61,134,899,001 (0.263%) higher, partly driven by exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

**YoY count dates.** Korea's YoY compares the end of September 2026 with the end of September 2025, both end-of-month stocks, under the [BOK count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** A later RBI supplement, of 23 Oct 2026, prints reserve money of 5,324,881 (₹ crore) on 15 October with a fortnight change of 46,739 "With respect to September 30, 2026". So 30 September = 5,324,881 − 46,739 = 5,278,142, the same as the [adopted figure](../issuers/RBI/2026.md#rbi-reserve-money-2026-09-30-wss-2026-10-09): no revision. Its own figure falls in October, outside the quarter, so it is a check only and is never summed.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 3,925.6 billion ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2026.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2026-09-25-published-2026-09-29)), agrees with the summed items (3,925,631 EUR millions) to its rounding.
- **Russia.** The narrow definition of the monetary base, 22,730.6 billion rubles on 1 October 2026 ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2026.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102026-retrieved-2026-11-05)), is a different definition, not a conflict. Required reserves are 496.1 in the narrow table and 587.6 in the broad file for the same date; the Bank of Russia's footnotes do not explain the difference, so it is recorded as unexplained. **FX divergence:** the RUB rate (82.8763164 RUB per USD, the Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB close × USD/CNY) is −0.60% from the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close (83.3774); at the ICE close the CBR figure would be $395,523,247,307, $2,391,397,461 less. The adopted rate follows the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule); the gap is flagged, not adjusted. Every other rate is within 0.08% of its TradingView ICE close.
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's October 2026 statistics tables prints 434,923 (R$ million) for 'set-2026*', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded, with currency in circulation 382,359 and bank reserves 52,563, which sum to 434,922 (rounding; read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-05)); its '*' marks September 2026 as preliminary, so the figure is provisional. The same page and table still print June 2026 unchanged and still preliminary, so 2026Q2 is not revised. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,459,651,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-05)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 2,090,204 (HK$ million) for 2026-09 as the adopted daily figure for 2026-09-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2026.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2026-09-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-05)); a cross-check, never summed. The HKD rate (7.8453 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (7.84522).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (801,894 AED million, Sep 2026, Statistical Bulletin September 2026, preliminary) is the printed total and equals its four printed components; every overlapping month, including the Jun 2026 figure counted for 2026Q2 (783,545 and its four components), is unchanged from the Statistical Bulletin August 2026 (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2026.md#cbuae-monetary-base-sep-2026-statistical-bulletin-september-2026-table-3-retrieved-2026-11-19)); a cross-check, never summed. The August 2026 reference-only extract is kept and is not used. The AED rate (3.6728 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (3.6728). 30 September 2026 was a Wednesday and a UAE working day, so AED uses that day's close (`same day`).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (7,504,266 NT$ millions, 2026M09, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals; the CBC's ERM-H End of Period Amount prints 75043 (NT$ hundred millions), the adopted figure rounded (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBC/2026.md#cbc-reserve-money-2026-09-30-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-05)); a cross-check, never summed. The TWD rate (31.874 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (31.874).
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (311,379.9 billion won, 202609, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total; its two printed components, currency in circulation 207,615.7 and central bank liabilities to depository corporations 103,764.3, add up to 311,380.0, 0.1 above the printed total, a rounding difference in the published components; the printed total is used. ECOS first printed 310,470 with no components on 11 November 2026 (kept in the [first-print extract](../issuers/BOK/2026.md#bok-monetary-base-2026-09-30-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-11)); the later vintage is adopted ([extract](../issuers/BOK/2026.md#bok-monetary-base-2026-09-30-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)). The Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures; a cross-check, never summed. The KRW rate (1356.54 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (1356.54).
- **Australia.** The adopted figure (292.1 $ billion, September 2026, RBA table D3 `DMAMMB` "Money base", monthly average, inferred) is the RBA's printed total; D3 prints no components, so there is no sum to check ([extract](../issuers/RBA/2026.md#rba-d3-money-base-2026-09-retrieved-2026-11-20)). No earlier D3 vintage that prints September 2026 is archived, so the adopted print is the only one on record. The AUD rate (0.711814 USD per AUD, the mean of 22 weekday Yahoo closes) is +0.057% from the TradingView ICE mean of the same weekdays (0.711412; denominator: the TradingView ICE mean).
- **Other published aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
