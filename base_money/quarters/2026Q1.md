## What's left out

**Banco Central do Brasil (BCB)** is not in the total. Its reserve money (series SGS 20306) is published, but we could not confirm from a BCB primary page whether it is an end-of-month or an average figure. For the coverage figure only, it is counted at BRL 1,372,502,000,000 (March 2026) × 0.19120075027174406632 USD per BRL (1 / [5.230105 BRL per USD](../FX/2026.md#fx-2026q1-brl)) = $262,423,412,149 from the [SGS values page](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-mar2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22) (the [SGS JSON API](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-mar2026-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-10-22) gives the same figure).

Coverage = $22,365,865,359,611 ÷ ($22,365,865,359,611 + $262,423,412,149) = 98.84028609146…%, shown as 98.84%. The basket's floor is 94%, so the quarter is published. (The eight issuers' individually rounded shares add up to 98.83%; the 0.01 gap is display rounding.)

**What this means for growth rates.** Because BCB is missing, this total is lower than the full basket by BCB's amount. A growth rate into or out of this quarter is only like-for-like against a quarter that also leaves BCB out. 2026Q2 does ($22,041,431,082,440, $324,434,277,171 or 1.451% lower than 2026Q1), so that comparison is fair within the same basket; part of it is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** Two later final RBI supplements print the same 5,107,831 for 31 March: [10 Jul 2026](../issuers/RBI/2026.md#rbi-reserve-money-2026-06-30-wss-2026-07-10) (the 2026Q2 source) and 23 Oct 2026, so no revision. The 5 Jun 2026 issue also prints 5,107,831 but is marked "Data are provisional", so it is not used.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,148.7 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,148,672 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2026.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2026-03-27-published-2026-03-31)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (19,974.5 billion rubles on 1 April) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2026.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01042026-retrieved-2026-10-23)). The two tables differ on required reserves (494.8 narrow vs 587.5 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in 2026Q2. Currency issued on 1 April (19,479.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Mar, 2026" row is the stock at the start of 1 April (likewise 19,178.6 on 1 March for "Feb, 2026").
- **Brazil.** BCB's narrow monetary base (SGS 1788, BRL 438,897,105,000 for March) is an alternative definition, never summed and not the reference value ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2026.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-mar2026-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-22)); it is 0.320 of SGS 20306.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The Fed's two components add to 5,458.5 against the printed 5,458.6 (the H.6 notes that components may not add up due to rounding); the PBOC's add to 408,745.67 against the printed 408,745.66. We use the printed totals. Every other printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
