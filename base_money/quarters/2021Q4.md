Click an issuer for the bank's basic facts, a USD figure for the exact quote it comes from, and a rate for the exchange-rate source (each issuer extract carries the original and archived links). FX rates are market closes on or just before each report's count date (each FX row says which), or the mean of that month's weekday closes for a monthly figure (BOE, SNB, BCB, RBA); see [FX](../FX/2021.md#2021q4).

## What's left out

Nothing: all fourteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 December, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of December, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of December, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of December, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)). Australia's figure is the RBA's own money base (notes and coins held by the private sector, banks' deposits with the RBA and other RBA liabilities to the private non-bank sector), December average (inferred), used as published (see the [RBA README](../issuers/RBA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 31 December 2021, the quarter-end date itself**: 31 December 2021 was a Friday, and before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end Friday is used. The figure comes from the supplement of 7 January 2022 and is observed and final, not adjusted.

**The euro area's statement date is 31 December 2021, the quarter-end date itself**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, and 31 December 2021 was a Friday and a TARGET business day, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) that statement (published 5 January 2022) is used.

**Hong Kong's count date is 31 December 2021**: 31 December 2021 was a Friday and not a Hong Kong general holiday, and the HKMA API returns a daily figure for that date, so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** This quarter's total is $2,292,297,268,192 or 8.810% above [2020Q4](2020Q4.md)'s ($26,018,170,361,725); part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. [2022Q1](2022Q1.md) also counts all fourteen issuers: its total ($28,100,284,429,031) is $210,183,200,886 or 0.742% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $154,577,939,392 or 0.543% below [2021Q3](2021Q3.md)'s ($28,465,045,569,309). This quarter is the year-ago base for [2022Q4](2022Q4.md) ($25,426,912,547,018), which is $2,883,555,082,899 or 10.185% below this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** India's YoY compares 31 December 2021 with 25 December 2020 (371 days), the ECB's compares the week to 31 December 2021 with the week to 25 December 2020, Hong Kong's compares 31 December 2021 with 31 December 2020, and Korea's compares the end of December 2021 with the end of December 2020, both end-of-month stocks; each follows its issuer's count-date rule.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The Federal Reserve figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (3,802,650 ₹ crore on 31 December) is the printed total; its printed components sum to 3,802,649, 1 below, a rounding difference. It is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2021 column (3,599,981) plus the printed 2021-22 "Financial Year so far" change (202,669) gives 3,802,650 ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2021.md#rbi-reserve-money-2021-12-31-wss-2022-01-07)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,836.1 billion, is the summed items (EUR 5,836,135 million) rounded to one decimal in billions ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2021.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2021-12-31-published-2022-01-05)).
- **United States.** The adopted figure (6,413.1, not seasonally adjusted monetary base, December average) is from the 23 May 2023 H.6 release, the last HTML release that still prints December 2021. The first print, in the 25 January 2022 release, was 6,413.3 (currency 2,225.3 + reserve balances 4,188.0). The printed total in the adopted vintage is 0.1 above its printed components (2,225.1 + 4,187.9 = 6,413.0), a rounding difference; the printed total is used ([extract](../issuers/FED/2021.md#fed-h6-monetary-base-2021-12-release-2023-05-23)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (14,553.8 billion rubles on 1 January 2022) is a different definition, not a conflict. Currency issued on 1 January 2022 (14,068.1) is the same in the narrow table, which confirms the broad "Dec, 2021" row is the stock at the start of 1 January. The printed broad total 20,338.9 is 0.1 below its printed components (14,068.1 + 2,650.6 + 815.3 + 2,805 = 20,339.0), a rounding difference; the printed total is used. The day before the start of 1 January, 31 December, has no Moscow Exchange trading (no rows from 31 December to 2 January), so the rate is the last close before it, Thursday 30 December. The ruble rate (74.67445575 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × the 30 December USD/CNY close, per the FX rule) is 0.0165% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (74.6868). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 74.68 on 30 December 2021, and the cross rate is 0.0074% below that close ([rate](../FX/2021.md#fx-2021q4-rub)).
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-December 2021 is 409,183,519 (R$ thousand). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", 820,263, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2021.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-dec2021-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-19)).
- **China.** The adopted figure (329,487.34 亿元) is the printed total; its printed components sum to 329,487.33, 0.01 below, a rounding difference ([extract](../issuers/PBOC/2021.md#pboc-reserve-money-2021-12-file-2025110510260596858)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (5,349,100 NT$ millions, 2021M12, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals; the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 53491 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2021M12, the same figure rounded ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2021.md#cbc-reserve-money-2021-12-31-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)). The TWD rate (27.7372 per USD) is 0.055% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (27.722); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (248,785.6 billion won, 202112, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total and equals its two printed components; the Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2021.md#bok-monetary-base-2021-12-31-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)); a cross-check, never summed. The rate is the 30 December close: 31 December 2021 was the Seoul foreign exchange market's year-end closing day, so under the holiday rule the last Seoul trading day's close is used (MarketWatch's 31 December close, 1,188.88, is not used). The KRW rate (1,189.96 per USD) is 0.050% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1,189.36) and 0.097% above the BOK's own Seoul close that day (1,188.8), measured against MarketWatch's close; the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and both gaps are flagged.
- **Australia.** The adopted figure (515.4 $ billion, December 2021, RBA table D3 `DMAMMB` "Money base", monthly average, inferred) is the RBA's printed total; D3 prints no components, so there is no sum to check ([extract](../issuers/RBA/2021.md#rba-d3-money-base-2021-12-retrieved-2026-11-20)). The four archived earlier D3 vintages that print December 2021 (the earliest published 28-Feb-2023) print the same figure, so no earlier print is superseded. The AUD rate (0.716239 USD per AUD, the mean of 23 weekday MarketWatch closes) is +0.024% from the TradingView ICE mean of the same weekdays (0.716069; denominator: the TradingView ICE mean).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except China's (0.01 亿元), India's (1 crore), the Fed's (0.1 billion dollars) and the CBR's (0.1 billion rubles), each noted above. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 23 weekday closes, 1 to 31 December). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 December 2021 was a Friday; the CNY, EUR, INR and HKD rates are that day's close, and the JPY, RUB, AED, TWD and KRW rates are the last close before it, Thursday 30 December, because those local markets were closed. 27 and 28 December were local-market holidays for GBP, so under the holiday rule those days take 24 December's close in the month average; 25 and 26 December were a Saturday and a Sunday, so CHF and BRL needed no weekday substitution. For AUD, New South Wales public holidays in the month average: 27 December (the additional Christmas holiday) and 28 December (the additional Boxing Day holiday) take 24 December's close (0.716326 without the substitution). The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2021.md#2021q4). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check, flagged here since there is no divergence limit: INR +0.062%; CNY +0.060%; EUR +0.058%; TWD +0.055%; KRW +0.050%; CHF +0.050%; BRL +0.031%; AUD +0.024%; GBP +0.021%; JPY +0.017%; RUB −0.0165%; AED +0.011%; HKD +0.009%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $28,311,239,659,277, 0.003% above the adopted total. The BOK's official Seoul close on 30 December (1,188.8) is 0.097% below the adopted MarketWatch close (against MarketWatch's close), also flagged.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
