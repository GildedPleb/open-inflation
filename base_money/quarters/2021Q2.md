## What's left out

Nothing: all thirteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 June, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of June, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of June, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of June, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 25 June 2021, not 30 June**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end, 25 June 2021, is used (30 June 2021 was a Wednesday). The figure comes from the supplement of 2 July 2021 and is observed, not adjusted to quarter end.

**The euro area's statement date is 25 June 2021, not 30 June**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last statement dated on or before the quarter end, as at 25 June 2021 (published 29 June), is used; the next one, as at 2 July 2021, is after the quarter end.

**Hong Kong's count date is 30 June 2021**: 30 June 2021 was a Wednesday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (Tuen Ng Festival was 14 June), and the HKMA API returns a daily figure for that date, so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** This quarter's total is $929,319,033,106 or 3.471% above [2021Q1](2021Q1.md)'s ($26,773,106,574,868); part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. [2021Q3](2021Q3.md) also counts all thirteen issuers: its total ($28,110,857,627,109) is $408,432,019,135 or 1.474% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2022Q2](2022Q2.md) ($25,890,228,341,322), which is $1,812,197,266,652 or 6.542% below this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** This page has no year-ago quarter, so there is no YoY count-date comparison here. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2022Q2](2022Q2.md), whose YoY count dates are noted there.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The Federal Reserve figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (3,698,930 ₹ crore on 25 June) is the printed total and equals its printed components. It is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2021 column (3,599,981) plus the printed 2021-22 "Financial Year so far" change (98,949) gives 3,698,930 ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2021.md#rbi-reserve-money-2021-06-25-wss-2021-07-02)). The next issue, dated 9 July 2021, prints a 2 July figure and a week variation that imply the same 3,698,930 on 25 June.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,855.7 billion, is the summed items (EUR 5,855,659 million) rounded to one decimal in billions ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2021.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2021-06-25-published-2021-06-29)).
- **United States.** The adopted figure (6,027.0, not seasonally adjusted monetary base, June average) is from the 22 November 2022 H.6 release, the last HTML release that still prints June 2021. The first print, in the 27 July 2021 release, was 6,027.1 (currency 2,179.1 + reserve balances 3,848.1 = 6,027.2, 0.1 above the printed total); it is kept as a not-adopted extract and named as superseded, under the FED latest-vintage rule. The printed total in the adopted vintage (6,027.0) is 0.1 below its printed components (2,179.0 + 3,848.1 = 6,027.1) ([extract](../issuers/FED/2021.md#fed-h6-monetary-base-2021-06-release-2022-11-22)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (13,989.0 billion rubles on 1 July 2021) is a different definition, not a conflict. Currency issued on 1 July 2021 (13,542.5) is the same in the narrow table, which confirms the broad "Jun, 2021" row is the stock at the start of 1 July. The printed broad total 19,298.1 equals its printed components (13,542.5 + 3,047.2 + 772.3 + 1,314.2 + 621.9). The ruble rate (73.13360148 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB 11.3259 × the 30 June USD/CNY close 6.4572, per the FX rule, unrounded product) is 0.006% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (73.1377; denominator: the TradingView ICE close). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 73.135 on 30 June 2021, and the cross rate is 0.002% below that close (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close) ([rate](../FX/2021.md#fx-2021q2-rub)).
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-June 2021 is 422,212,123 (R$ thousand). The SGS JSON shows no components. Cross-check, read, not extracted: Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 monetary and credit statistics tables prints 422,212 (R$ million) for 'jun-2021', unmarked, with currency in circulation 340,096 and bank reserves 82,117 (340,096 + 82,117 = 422,213, 1 above the printed 422,212, rounding); the adopted figure rests on the archived SGS JSON ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2021.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2021-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-19)).
- **China.** The adopted figure (324,494.14 亿元) is the printed total and equals its printed components ([extract](../issuers/PBOC/2021.md#pboc-reserve-money-2021-06-file-2025110510260596858)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (5,103,767 NT$ millions, 2021M06, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2021.md#cbc-reserve-money-2021-06-30-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)). Cross-check, read, not summed: the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 51,038 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2021.06, the adopted figure rounded. The TWD rate (27.9132 per USD) is 0.169% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (27.866; denominator: the TradingView ICE close); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (242,801.1 billion won, 202106, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total and equals its two printed components; the Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2021.md#bok-monetary-base-2021-06-30-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)); a cross-check, never summed. The KRW rate (1,130.62 per USD) is 0.006% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1,130.55; denominator: the TradingView ICE close) and 0.400% above the BOK's own Seoul close that day (1,126.1; denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,130.62); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and both gaps are flagged.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except the Federal Reserve's (0.1 billion dollars in both the first print and the adopted vintage) and Table 28's 422,212 (R$ million), 1 below its printed components, each noted above. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 22 weekday closes, 1 to 30 June; 3 June, Brazil's Corpus Christi, takes 2 June's close in the BRL average). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 June 2021 was a Wednesday; the CNY, JPY, HKD, AED, TWD, KRW and RUB rates are that day's close (RUB as the last close before the start of 1 July), and the EUR and INR rates are Friday 25 June. GBP and CHF needed no weekday substitution. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2021.md#2021q2). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check are (MarketWatch − TradingView) / TradingView ICE close, flagged here and in each [FX row](../FX/2021.md#2021q2) with its denominator, since there is no divergence limit: INR +0.196%; TWD +0.169%; EUR +0.042%; CHF +0.033%; JPY +0.027%; GBP +0.018%; BRL +0.018%; CNY +0.009%; AED +0.008%; KRW +0.006%; RUB −0.006%; HKD −0.009%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $27,702,873,496,176, 0.002% above the adopted total. The BOK's official Seoul close on 30 June (1,126.1) is 0.400% below the adopted MarketWatch close (denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,130.62), also flagged. The adopted ruble cross is 0.002% below the Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB close that day (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close 73.135).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
