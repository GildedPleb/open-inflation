## What's left out

Nothing: all twelve basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 September, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of September, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of September, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 30 September 2022, the last Friday of the quarter**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end is used. The figure comes from the supplement of 7 October 2022 and is observed, not adjusted; the quarter-end date itself is a Friday, so it is the count date.

**Hong Kong's count date is 30 September 2022**: 30 September 2022 was a Friday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (the Mid-Autumn Festival substitute holiday was 12 September), so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** [2022Q4](2022Q4.md) also counts all twelve issuers: its total ($24,827,420,587,476) is $822,404,533,718 or 3.426% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2023Q3](2023Q3.md) ($23,736,449,615,198), which is $268,566,438,560 or 1.119% below this quarter's.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The UAE figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,129,699 ₹ crore on 30 September) is the printed total and equals its printed components. Consistency check within the same supplement: the 31 March 2022 column (4,068,887) plus the printed 2022-23 "Financial Year so far" change (60,813) gives 4,129,700, 1 above the printed Sep. 30 column, a rounding difference. The next supplement (14 October 2022 figures) prints 4,095,764 with a week change of −33,936, which implies 4,129,700 for 30 September, 1 above the adopted figure; no supplement found prints a revised 30 September level, so the difference is recorded as unexplained, the printed figure is used unchanged and no revision is appended ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2022.md#rbi-reserve-money-2022-09-30-wss-2022-10-07)). The RBI may have revised this Friday's figure later: the 6 October 2023 supplement's year-on-year column implies 4,129,912 for 30 September 2022 (4,395,058 less 265,146), 213 crore (0.005%) above the adopted 4,129,699. We keep the figure first published, in the 7 October 2022 supplement, which is final and unmarked; the later implied value changes no displayed YoY (+6.4% either way).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 6,262.5 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 6,262,472 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2022.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2022-09-30-published-2022-10-05)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (15,265.4 billion rubles on 1 October 2022) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2022.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102022-retrieved-2026-11-09)). The two tables differ on required reserves (107.0 narrow vs 145.9 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 October 2022 (15,158.4) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Sep, 2022" row is the stock at the start of 1 October. The day before the start of 1 October, 30 September, was a Friday with Moscow Exchange trading, so the rate is that day's close. The ruble rate (59.354556 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is **0.261% above** the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (59.2), the largest FX gap this quarter. Both legs are from the same day (the USD/CNY leg is the CNY row's 30 September close, 7.1160). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 58.45 on 30 September 2022, 1.27% below TradingView, and the cross rate is 1.55% above that close ([rate](../FX/2022.md#fx-2022q3-rub)). Converted at the TradingView close instead, Russia's figure would be $356,342,905,405 rather than $355,415,008,075. The product's standing rule is the CNY/RUB MOEX cross, so the MarketWatch-leg cross is used and the gap is flagged.
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-September 2022 is 393,440,715 (R$ thousand). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 926,176,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2022.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2022-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-09)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,931,566 (HK$ million) for 2022-09 as the adopted daily figure for 2022-09-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2022.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2022-09-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-09)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (461,467 AED million, Sep 2022, Statistical Bulletin November 2024, the latest bulletin still served by the CBUAE that prints September 2022) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. The September 2022 bulletin first printed 466,567, marked "* Preliminary", with the same Currency Issued, Reserve Requirements and Monetary Bills but current accounts and overnight deposits of 85,541 rather than 80,441. The figure was revised down by AED 5,100 million (1.1%), all in that line, between the first print and the November 2024 bulletin; the CBUAE gives no reason, so the revision is recorded as unexplained, and the later, unmarked figure is used under the [CBUAE vintage rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions). The September 2022 bulletin is a read cross-check, never summed (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2022.md#cbuae-monetary-base-sep-2022-statistical-bulletin-november-2024-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-09)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (5,669,275 NT$ millions, 2022M09, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals; the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 56693 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2022M09, the same figure rounded ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2022.md#cbc-reserve-money-2022-09-30-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-09)). The TWD rate (31.8175 per USD) is 0.046% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (31.803); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except India's FY-so-far check (off by 1 crore, noted above) and the Fed's (2,279.5 + 3,131.4 = 5,410.9). The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, also equals its components (15,158.4 + 107.0 = 15,265.4).
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 22 weekday closes, 1 to 30 September). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 September 2022 was a Friday, so every single-day rate is that day's close. 19 September was a UK bank holiday for the State Funeral of Queen Elizabeth II, so under the holiday rule it takes 16 September's close in the GBP month average; 7 September was a Brazilian national holiday, so it takes 6 September's close in the BRL month average (5.237105; 5.236909 without the substitution); there was no September holiday for CHF. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2022.md#2022q3). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check, flagged here since there is no divergence limit: RUB +0.261% (above); INR +0.072%; TWD +0.046%; CHF +0.043%; CNY +0.035%; GBP +0.027%; BRL +0.021%; EUR +0.017%; AED +0.016%; JPY −0.006%; HKD +0.001%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $24,006,783,399,660, 0.007% above the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
