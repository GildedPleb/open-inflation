## What's left out

This total leaves out RBI and BCB. The CSV value is lower by the amount of the missing issuers, so growth rates into and out of this quarter are distorted and should not be read as changes in base money.

**Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** is not in the total. Our rule uses only the RBI's final figure for the quarter-end date, and there is none for 30 September 2025: until 15 December 2025 the RBI's Weekly Statistical Supplement published reserve money for Fridays only, so the nearest figures are 26 September and 3 October. Nothing is estimated or carried over. For the coverage figure only, it is counted at its latest figure inside the quarter, INR 48,715,290,000,000 (26 September 2025) × 0.01128286133363420964 USD per INR (1 / [88.63 INR per USD](../FX/2025.md#fx-2025q3-inr)) = $549,647,861,898 from the [WSS of 3 October 2025](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-09-26-reference-only-wss-2025-10-03).

**Banco Central do Brasil (BCB)** is not in the total. Its reserve money (series SGS 20306) is published, but we could not confirm from a BCB primary page whether it is an end-of-month or an average figure. For the coverage figure only, it is counted at BRL 1,361,797,000,000 (September 2025) × 0.18632872598292595352 USD per BRL (1 / [5.366859 BRL per USD](../FX/2025.md#fx-2025q3-brl)) = $253,741,900,057 from the [SGS values page](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23) (the [SGS JSON API](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2025-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-10-23) gives the same figure).

Coverage = $22,252,821,403,725 ÷ ($22,252,821,403,725 + $549,647,861,898 + $253,741,900,057) = 96.51551698506…%, shown as 96.52%. The basket's floor is 94%, so the quarter is published.

**What this means for growth rates.** Because RBI and BCB are missing, this total is lower than the full basket by their amounts. A growth rate into or out of this quarter is only like-for-like against a quarter that leaves out the same two banks. [2025Q4](2025Q4.md) leaves out only BCB, so its total ($22,472,588,647,198, $219,767,243,473 higher) is not comparable with this one: it includes India ($534,234,459,881 in 2025Q4), which is more than that gap. Over the seven banks in both quarters, 2025Q4's figures sum to $21,938,354,187,317, $314,467,216,408 or 1.413% below this quarter; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The reference figure (4,871,529 ₹ crore on 26 September) is consistent with the next supplement, 10 Oct 2025 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-09-26-reference-only-wss-2025-10-03)), which prints 4,855,933 for 3 October and a week change of -15,596, implying the same figure for 26 September.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,374.2 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,374,240 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2025.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2025-09-26-published-2025-09-30)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,846.3 billion rubles on 1 October 2025) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2025.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102025-retrieved-2026-10-23)). The two tables differ on required reserves (452.4 narrow vs 554.8 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 October (18,393.9) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Sep, 2025" row is the stock at the start of 1 October (likewise 18,232.0 on 1 September for "Aug, 2025").
- **Brazil.** BCB's narrow monetary base (SGS 1788, BRL 429,700,158,000 for September) is an alternative definition, never summed and not the reference value ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23)); it is 0.316 of SGS 20306.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
