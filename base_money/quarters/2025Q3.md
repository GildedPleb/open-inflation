## What's left out

Nothing: all nine basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 26 September 2025, not 30 September**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before quarter end is used.

**Growth rates.** [2025Q4](2025Q4.md) also counts all nine issuers: its total ($22,554,451,077,005) is $328,083,671,613 or 1.434% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $535,711,499,311 (2.288%) lower than [2025Q2](2025Q2.md)'s ($23,418,246,247,929).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,871,529 ₹ crore on 26 September) is consistent with the next supplement, 10 Oct 2025 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-09-26-wss-2025-10-03)), which prints 4,855,933 for 3 October and a week change of -15,596, implying the same figure for 26 September.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,374.2 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,374,240 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2025.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2025-09-26-published-2025-09-30)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,846.3 billion rubles on 1 October 2025) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2025.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102025-retrieved-2026-10-23)). The two tables differ on required reserves (452.4 narrow vs 554.8 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 October (18,393.9) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Sep, 2025" row is the stock at the start of 1 October (likewise 18,232.0 on 1 September for "Aug, 2025").
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 429,700 (R$ million) for 'set-2025', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted, because no archive could capture the workbook; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,361,797,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23)).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.

New sources were read and saved to the Wayback Machine on 23 Oct 2026 (6:31–6:36 PM ET), and each snapshot was checked to show the figure used. The CBR broad-base spreadsheet is the same document as for 2025Q4 and 2026, captured again. archive.today and Ghostarchive copies were made in a browser the same evening, with each CAPTCHA solved (Ghostarchive 6:37–7:01 PM ET), and each copy was opened to confirm its content; each Ghostarchive copy's saved file was also checked for the figure it backs. Neither archive could capture the CBR broad-base spreadsheet, as in earlier quarters, and archive.today stored a BCB rejection page instead of the SGS 20306 values page, so that copy is recorded as a failed attempt. Each extract records its own archive links, capture checks and failed attempts (each failed attempt is a `- Archive attempt` line under its extract). FX rates are supporting data and are not archived.

BCB's SGS extracts keep the captures made when they were first read. On 26 Oct 2026 the BCB definition sources and current SGS pages were read and saved in a browser where the archives allowed (archive URLs in the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)).
