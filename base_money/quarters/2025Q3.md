## What's left out

Nothing: all eleven basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 September, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of September, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 26 September 2025, not 30 September**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before quarter end is used.

**Growth rates.** [2025Q4](2025Q4.md) also counts all eleven issuers: its total ($23,060,495,162,642) is $308,307,760,114 or 1.319% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $553,711,728,165 (2.315%) lower than [2025Q2](2025Q2.md)'s ($23,922,514,650,921).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,871,529 ₹ crore on 26 September) is consistent with the next supplement, 10 Oct 2025 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/RBI/2025.md#rbi-reserve-money-2025-09-26-wss-2025-10-03)), which prints 4,855,933 for 3 October and a week change of -15,596, implying the same figure for 26 September.
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,374.2 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,374,240 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2025.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2025-09-26-published-2025-09-30)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,846.3 billion rubles on 1 October 2025) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2025.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102025-retrieved-2026-10-23)). The two tables differ on required reserves (452.4 narrow vs 554.8 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 October (18,393.9) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Sep, 2025" row is the stock at the start of 1 October (likewise 18,232.0 on 1 September for "Aug, 2025").
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 429,700 (R$ million) for 'set-2025', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-26)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,361,797,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2025.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2025-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-23)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 2,020,238 (HK$ million) for 2025-09 as the adopted daily figure for 2025-09-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2025.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2025-09-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-28)); a cross-check, never summed. The HKD rate (7.782 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (7.782).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (832,515 AED million, Sep 2025, Statistical Bulletin August 2026) is the printed total and equals its four printed components; the CBUAE's "UAE Monetary Base (Monthly)" release for February 2026 prints 832.5 (AED billion) for Sep-25, the adopted 832,515 (AED million) rounded; the Statistical Bulletins of February and June 2026 print the same 832,515 (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2025.md#cbuae-monetary-base-sep-2025-statistical-bulletin-august-2026-table-3-retrieved-2026-10-29)); a cross-check, never summed. The AED rate (3.6729 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (3.6729).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
