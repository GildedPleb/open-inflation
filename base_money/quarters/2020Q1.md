## What's left out

Nothing: all fourteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 March, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Certificates of Deposit / Monetary Bill), end of March, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of March, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of March, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)). Australia's figure is the RBA's own money base (notes and coins held by the private sector, banks' deposits with the RBA and other RBA liabilities to the private non-bank sector), March average (inferred), used as published (see the [RBA README](../issuers/RBA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 31 March 2020**: the RBI publishes a final reserve-money figure for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) that date is used, even though 31 March 2020 was a Tuesday rather than a Friday. The figure comes from the supplement of 2 October 2020, issued after the RBI's 2019–20 annual accounts, and is observed and final, not interpolated or adjusted.

**The euro area's statement date is 27 March 2020, not 31 March**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last statement dated on or before the quarter end, as at 27 March 2020 (published 31 March), is used; the next one, as at 3 April 2020 (published 8 April), is after the quarter end.

**Hong Kong's count date is 31 March 2020**: 31 March 2020 was a Tuesday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (Ching Ming Festival was Saturday 4 April and Good Friday was 10 April; see the [HKMA extract](../issuers/HKMA/2020.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2020-03-31-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-20) and [FX notes](../FX/2020.md#2020q1) for the archived 2020 gazette), and the HKMA API returns a daily figure for that date, so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** [2020Q2](2020Q2.md) also counts all fourteen issuers: its total ($22,524,880,685,150) is $2,981,459,364,386 or 15.256% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q1](2021Q1.md) ($26,999,496,221,468), which is $7,456,074,900,704 or 38.151% above this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** This page has no year-ago quarter, so there is no YoY count-date comparison here. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q1](2021Q1.md), whose YoY count dates are noted there.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The Federal Reserve figure was revised down from its first H.6 print, and the RBI figure was revised up after its 2019–20 annual accounts.

- **India.** The adopted figure (3,029,707 ₹ crore on 31 March, WSS dated 2 October 2020) is the printed total and equals its printed components (2,447,312 + 543,888 + 38,507 = 3,029,707). Earlier WSS issues through 25 September 2020 printed 3,029,674 ₹ crore (currency in circulation 2,447,279; kept in a not-adopted extract and named as superseded). Consistency check against a later issue, read, not adopted: the WSS dated 1 January 2021 also prints the Mar. 31 column as 3,029,707 with the same three components ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2020.md#rbi-reserve-money-2020-03-31-wss-2020-10-02)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 3,334.6 billion, is the summed items (EUR 3,334,578 million) rounded to one decimal in billions ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2020.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2020-03-27-published-2020-03-31)).
- **United States.** The adopted figure (3,883.1, not seasonally adjusted monetary base, March average) is from the 24 August 2021 H.6 release, the last HTML release that still prints March 2020 (the 28 September 2021 release drops March 2020). The first H.6 print, in the 24 September 2020 release (when H.6 added Table 1 Monetary base following the discontinuation of H.3 on 17 September 2020), was 3,883.2 (currency 1,838.0 + reserve balances 2,045.1 = 3,883.1, 0.1 below the printed total, rounding; consistent with the 9 July 2020 H.3 release's 3,883,155 million dollars); it is kept as a not-adopted extract and named as superseded, under the FED latest-vintage rule. The printed total in the adopted vintage (3,883.1) equals its printed components (1,838.0 + 2,045.1 = 3,883.1) ([extract](../issuers/FED/2020.md#fed-h6-monetary-base-2020-03-release-2021-08-24)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (11,536.7 billion rubles on 1 April 2020) is a different definition, not a conflict. Currency issued on 1 April 2020 (11,167.5) is the same in the narrow table, which confirms the broad "Mar, 2020" row is the stock at the start of 1 April. The printed broad total 17,771.1 equals its printed components (11,167.5 + 2,615.9 + 621.6 + 1,835.7 + 1,530.4). The ruble rate (78.2817672 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB 11.053 × the 31 March USD/CNY close 7.0824, per the FX rule, unrounded product) is 0.305% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (78.521; denominator: the TradingView ICE close). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 78.3725 on 31 March 2020, and the cross rate is 0.116% below that close (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close) ([rate](../FX/2020.md#fx-2020q1-rub)).
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-March 2020 is 307,560,246 (R$ thousand). The SGS JSON shows no components. Cross-check, read, not extracted: Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 monetary and credit statistics tables prints 307,560 (R$ million) for 'mar-2020', unmarked, with currency in circulation 259,536 and banking reserves 48,024 (259,536 + 48,024 = 307,560, equal to the printed total); the adopted figure rests on the archived SGS JSON ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2020.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-mar2020-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-20)).
- **China.** The adopted figure (317,806.72 亿元) is the printed total and equals its printed components (90,750.94 + 212,680.93 + 14,374.85 = 317,806.72) ([extract](../issuers/PBOC/2020.md#pboc-reserve-money-2020-03-file-2025110510251391352)).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (415,177 AED millions, Mar 2020, May 2022 bulletin Table 4) is the printed total and equals its four printed components (105,200 + 132,583 + 32,816 + 144,578 = 415,177). Cross-check, read, not adopted: the April 2022 bulletin prints the same 415,177 and the same components for Mar 2020 ([extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2020.md#cbuae-monetary-base-mar-2020-statistical-bulletin-may-2022-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-20)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (4,430,778 NT$ millions, 2020M03, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2020.md#cbc-reserve-money-2020-03-31-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-20)). Cross-check, read, not summed: the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 44,308 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2020.03, the adopted figure rounded. The TWD rate (30.2830 per USD) is 0.341% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (30.18; denominator: the TradingView ICE close); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (194,031.8 billion won, 202003, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total and is 0.1 below its two printed components (118,025.1 + 76,006.8 = 194,031.9); the Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2020.md#bok-monetary-base-2020-03-31-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-20)); a cross-check, never summed. The KRW rate (1,218.80 per USD) is 0.092% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1,217.68; denominator: the TradingView ICE close) and 0.115% above the BOK's own Seoul close that day (1,217.4; denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,218.80); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and both gaps are flagged.
- **Australia.** The adopted figure (137.2 $ billion, March 2020, RBA table D3 `DMAMMB` "Money base", monthly average, inferred) is the RBA's printed total; D3 prints no components, so there is no sum to check ([extract](../issuers/RBA/2020.md#rba-d3-money-base-2020-03-retrieved-2026-11-20)). The six archived earlier D3 vintages that print March 2020 (the earliest published 29-May-2020) print the same figure, so no earlier print is superseded. The AUD rate (0.621268 USD per AUD, the mean of 22 weekday MarketWatch closes) is −0.045% from the TradingView ICE mean of the same weekdays (0.621548; denominator: the TradingView ICE mean).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except the BOK's (194,031.8, 0.1 billion won below its printed components), noted above. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 22 weekday closes, 1 to 31 March; no weekday substitution). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 March 2020 was a Tuesday; the CNY, JPY, INR, HKD, AED, TWD, KRW and RUB rates are that day's close (RUB as the prior close before the start of 1 April), and the EUR rate is Friday 27 March. GBP, CHF and BRL needed no weekday substitution. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2020.md#2020q1). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check are (MarketWatch − TradingView) / TradingView ICE close, flagged here and in each [FX row](../FX/2020.md#2020q1) with its denominator, since there is no divergence limit: TWD +0.341%; RUB −0.305%; KRW +0.092%; GBP +0.060%; AUD −0.045%; INR +0.040%; CHF +0.024%; JPY +0.021%; BRL +0.014%; AED +0.014%; EUR −0.009%; HKD +0.002%; CNY +0.000%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $19,544,694,682,439, 0.007% above the adopted total. The BOK's official Seoul close on 31 March (1,217.4) is 0.115% below the adopted MarketWatch close (denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,218.80), also flagged. The adopted ruble cross is 0.116% below the Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB close that day (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close 78.3725).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
