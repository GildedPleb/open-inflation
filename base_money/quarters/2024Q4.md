## What's left out

Nothing: all ten basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 December, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 27 December 2024, not 31 December**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before quarter end is used.

**Growth rates.** [2025Q1](2025Q1.md) also counts all ten issuers: its total ($23,077,389,072,203) is $621,098,253,047 or 2.766% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $1,443,259,438,586 (6.039%) lower than [2024Q3](2024Q3.md)'s ($23,899,550,257,742).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,662,826 ₹ crore on 27 December) is the printed total; its printed components sum to 4,662,827, 1 off, a rounding difference. The next weekly issue (10 January 2025) implies 4,662,827 from its printed week change, 1 off, so no revision is seen ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2024.md#rbi-reserve-money-2024-12-27-wss-2025-01-03)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,648.1 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,648,092 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2024.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2024-12-27-published-2024-12-31)).
- **Japan.** The BOJ's December 2024 monthly release (base2412.pdf, dated 7 January 2025) prints the same end-of-period figures as the adopted long-series spreadsheet; the BOJ site no longer serves that PDF, so it is cited from existing Wayback captures as a cross-check in the [extract](../issuers/BOJ/2024.md#boj-monetary-base-end-of-period-2024-12-mblongxlsx-retrieved-2026-10-26).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,889.7 billion rubles on 1 January 2025) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2024.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01012025-retrieved-2026-10-28)). The two tables differ on required reserves (378.4 narrow vs 499.3 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 January (18,511.2) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Dec, 2024" row is the stock at the start of 1 January (likewise 17,846.0 on 1 December for "Nov, 2024"). The Moscow Exchange did not trade CNY/RUB on 31 December 2024, so the last close before, 30 December, is used. The ruble rate (100.0301 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is 9.5% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (110.4702); the FX rule's Moscow Exchange route is used as written ([FX](../FX/2024.md#fx-2024q4-rub)).
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 451,214 (R$ million) for 'dez-2024', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-dec2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-28)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,304,624,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-dec2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-28)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,957,503 (HK$ million) for 2024-12 as the adopted daily figure for 2024-12-31, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2024.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2024-12-31-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-28)); a cross-check, never summed. The HKD rate (7.7675 per USD) matches the TradingView ICE close (7.7675).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The CBR's broad-base components for December 2024 sum to 27,958.3 billion rubles, 0.1 below the printed total 27,958.4; the RBI's components sum to 1 ₹ crore above its printed total; the printed totals are used. Every other printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX data gaps.** Yahoo Finance has no BRL bar for 25 December 2024, so the BRL December average uses 21 weekday closes (TradingView ICE has 22). On 31 December 2024 the last USD/CNY bar is at 16:00 UTC, which is the close used.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
