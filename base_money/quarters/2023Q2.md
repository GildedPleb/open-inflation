## What's left out

Nothing: all eleven basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 June, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of June, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 30 June 2023, the quarter-end date itself**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end is used, and 30 June 2023 was a Friday. The figure comes from the supplement of 7 July 2023 and is observed, not adjusted.

**Growth rates.** [2023Q3](2023Q3.md) also counts all eleven issuers: its total ($23,546,473,548,099) is $704,634,871,030 or 2.906% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source used now prints a figure that disagrees with an adopted one. The India check below finds a small unexplained difference implied by the next supplement, and the UAE figure was revised down from its first, preliminary print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,370,091 ₹ crore on 30 June) is the printed total; its printed components add up to 4,370,090, 1 below it, a rounding difference in the published components. It is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2023 column (4,386,759) plus the printed 2023-24 "Financial Year so far" change (−16,668) gives 4,370,091. The next supplement (7 July 2023 figures, published 14 July) prints 4,363,556 with a week change of −6,768, which implies 4,370,324 for 30 June, 233 (₹ crore, 0.005%) above the adopted figure, all in currency in circulation; the year-on-year change in the supplement of 5 July 2024 implies the same 4,370,324. No supplement found prints a revised 30 June level, so the difference is recorded as unexplained, the printed figure is used unchanged and no revision is appended ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2023.md#rbi-reserve-money-2023-06-30-wss-2023-07-07)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,317.4 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 5,317,391 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2023.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2023-06-30-published-2023-07-05)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (17,998.0 billion rubles on 1 July 2023) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2023.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01072023-retrieved-2026-11-03)). The two tables differ on required reserves (200.5 narrow vs 271.3 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 July 2023 (17,797.4) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Jun, 2023" row is the stock at the start of 1 July (likewise 17,338.4 on 1 June for "May, 2023"). The day before the start of 1 July, 30 June, was a Friday and a Moscow Exchange trading day (no rows for 1 and 2 July, a weekend), so the rate is the 30 June close. The ruble rate (89.450162 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is **0.70% above** the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (88.8249), the largest FX gap this quarter. Both legs are from the same day (the USD/CNY leg is the CNY row's 30 June close, 7.2535). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 89.55 on 30 June 2023, 0.82% above TradingView, and the cross rate is 0.11% below that close ([rate](../FX/2023.md#fx-2023q2-rub)). Converted at the TradingView close instead, Russia's figure would be $280,655,536,905 rather than $278,693,737,860.
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 403,119 (R$ million) for 'jun-2023', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded, with currency in circulation 333,116 and bank reserves 70,003 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-jun2023-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-03)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. The SGS values page answered, so it is the adopted source, and the SGS JSON API response for the same series, archived, prints the same figure (see the extract). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,028,002,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-jun2023-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-03)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,871,052 (HK$ million) for 2023-06 as the adopted daily figure for 2023-06-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2023.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2023-06-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-03)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (611,344 AED million, Jun 2023, Statistical Bulletin August 2025, the latest bulletin still served by the CBUAE that prints June 2023; from the September 2025 bulletin on, the table no longer prints June 2023) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. The June 2023 bulletin first printed 622,144, marked "* Preliminary", with the same Currency Issued, Reserve Requirements and Monetary Bills but current accounts and overnight deposits of 109,691 rather than 98,891. The figure was revised down by AED 10,800 million (1.7%), all in that line, between the first print and the August 2025 bulletin; the CBUAE gives no reason, so the revision is recorded as unexplained, and the later, unmarked figure is used under the [CBUAE vintage rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions). The June 2023 bulletin is a read cross-check, never summed (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2023.md#cbuae-monetary-base-jun-2023-statistical-bulletin-august-2025-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-03)).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The printed totals are used. The Fed's equals its components (2,342.5 + 3,265.7 = 5,608.2 billion dollars), and so do the BoJ's, the SNB's, the HKMA's and the CBUAE's. Three are a rounding step above their printed components: the PBOC's by 0.01 (its unit is 100 million yuan, so 1 million yuan), the RBI's by 1 (₹ crore) and the CBR broad table's by 0.1 (17,797.4 + 4,105.3 + 271.3 + 2,755.1 = 24,929.1 vs 24,929.2, billion rubles). The BCB's SGS page shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, prints 17,998.0, 0.1 above its components (17,797.4 + 200.5 = 17,997.9).
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 22 weekday closes, 1 to 30 June). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 June 2023 was a Friday, so the CNY, EUR, JPY, INR and HKD rates are the 30 June close, and so is the RUB rate (the last close before the start of 1 July). The AED rate is the 26 June close: the UAE financial markets were closed from 27 to 30 June for Eid Al-Adha, so under the holiday rule it is the last local business day's close (MarketWatch's 30 June close, 3.6730, is not used). 8 June was a Brazilian holiday (Corpus Christi), so under the holiday rule it takes 7 June's close in the BRL month average (4.849386; 4.849277 without the substitution); the UK's and Switzerland's nearest holidays, 29 May, fall outside June, so there was no June holiday for GBP or CHF. The holiday calendars are cited, archived, in the [FX notes](../FX/2023.md#2023q2). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check, flagged here since there is no divergence limit: RUB +0.70% (above); CHF 0.900509 vs 0.899573, +0.104%; INR 82.10 vs 82.03, +0.085%; EUR 1.0914 vs 1.09074, +0.061%; JPY 144.32 vs 144.261, +0.041%; CNY 7.2535 vs 7.2511, +0.033%; GBP 1.263682 vs 1.263336, +0.027%; AED 3.6731 vs 3.6726, +0.014%; BRL 4.849386 vs 4.848805, +0.012%; HKD 7.8367 vs 7.83618, +0.007%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $24,253,952,500,695, 0.012% above the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
