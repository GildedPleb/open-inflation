## What's left out

Nothing: all ten basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 28 March, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 31 March 2024, the quarter-end date itself**: the RBI publishes a final reserve-money figure for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) that date is used rather than the last Friday (29 March). The figure comes from the supplement of 5 July 2024, issued after the RBI balance sheet (by end-May), so it is final.

**Hong Kong's count date is 28 March 2024, not 31 March**: 31 March 2024 was a Sunday, and 29 and 30 March (Good Friday and the day following Good Friday) were Hong Kong general holidays; the HKMA publishes no daily figure for any of the three dates (the API returns "No result found"), so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last business day before the quarter-end date is used.

**Growth rates.** [2024Q2](2024Q2.md) also counts all ten issuers: its total ($23,051,837,142,535) is $722,072,290,412 or 3.037% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,631,446 ₹ crore on 31 March) is the printed total and equals its printed components, and it is the base of the 2024-25 "Financial Year so far" change printed in the same supplement: 4,694,929 on 28 June less the printed change 63,483 gives 4,631,446, the same figure ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2024.md#rbi-reserve-money-2024-03-31-wss-2024-07-05)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,929.8 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,929,783 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2024.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2024-03-29-published-2024-04-04)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,063.8 billion rubles on 1 April 2024) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2024.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01042024-retrieved-2026-10-29)). The two tables differ on required reserves (382.8 narrow vs 504.3 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 April (17,680.9) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Mar, 2024" row is the stock at the start of 1 April (likewise 17,753.8 on 1 March for "Feb, 2024"). The day before the start of 1 April, 31 March, was a Sunday with no Moscow Exchange trading (no rows for 30 or 31 March), so the rate is the last close before it, Friday 29 March. The ruble rate (91.4998252 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is 1.18% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (92.5888), the largest FX gap this quarter. The gap comes from the CNY cross, not from the market quoted: the Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) traded on 29 March 2024 and closed at 92.48, 0.12% below TradingView, and the cross rate is 1.06% below that close ([rate](../FX/2024.md#fx-2024q1-rub)).
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's October 2026 statistics tables prints 417,796 (R$ million) for 'mar-2024', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-mar2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-29)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,172,013,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-mar2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-29)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,917,534 (HK$ million) for 2024-03 as the adopted daily figure for 2024-03-28, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2024.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2024-03-28-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-29)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components (the Fed's 2,339.9 + 3,543.1 = 5,883.0 billion dollars); the BCB's SGS page shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, prints 18,063.8 against components summing to 18,063.7, a 0.1 rounding difference.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 21 weekday closes, 1 to 29 March). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 March 2024 was a Sunday, so the CNY, JPY and RUB rates are the last close before it, Friday 29 March. Friday 29 March was Good Friday, a local-market holiday for EUR, INR, GBP, CHF and BRL (and, with 30 March, in Hong Kong), so under the holiday rule the EUR and INR rates are the 28 March close, and in the GBP, CHF and BRL month averages 29 March takes 28 March's close; the holiday calendars are cited, archived, in the [FX notes](../FX/2024.md#2024q1). The largest gap to the TradingView ICE cross-check is RUB (above); among the other rates it is INR (83.36 vs 83.332, 0.03%); converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $23,773,170,017,617, 0.003% below the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
