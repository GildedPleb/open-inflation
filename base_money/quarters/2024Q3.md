## What's left out

Nothing: all ten basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 September, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 27 September 2024, not 30 September**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before quarter end is used.

**Growth rates.** [2024Q4](2024Q4.md) also counts all ten issuers: its total ($22,456,290,819,156) is $1,443,259,438,586 or 6.039% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,660,654 ₹ crore on 27 September) is the printed total and equals its printed components. The next weekly issue (11 October 2024) implies 4,660,653 from its printed week change, 1 off, a rounding difference, so no revision is seen ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2024.md#rbi-reserve-money-2024-09-27-wss-2024-10-04)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,707.9 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 4,707,919 million) after rounding ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2024.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2024-09-27-published-2024-10-01)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,266.4 billion rubles on 1 October 2024) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2024.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102024-retrieved-2026-10-28)). The two tables differ on required reserves (382.8 narrow vs 504.0 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 October (17,883.6) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Sep, 2024" row is the stock at the start of 1 October (likewise 17,913.4 on 1 September for "Aug, 2024"). The last close before the start of 1 October is 30 September. The ruble rate (92.8164 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is 0.04% below the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (92.851) ([FX](../FX/2024.md#fx-2024q3-rub)).
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 429,859 (R$ million) for 'set-2024', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-28)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,278,790,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2024.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2024-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-10-28)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,941,425 (HK$ million) for 2024-09 as the adopted daily figure for 2024-09-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2024.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2024-09-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-10-28)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** The PBOC's components for September 2024 sum to 379130.08 (100 million yuan), 0.01 above the printed total 379130.07; the CBR's broad-base components sum to 28,531.4 billion rubles, 0.1 below the printed total 28,531.5; the printed totals are used. Every other printed total equals its components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: daily closes, not the rule's hourly closes.** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so the FX rule's hourly close at or before 22:00 UTC cannot be read for September 2024. Every rate here is instead Yahoo's daily bar close for the date (month averages: the mean of 21 weekday daily closes, the same count as TradingView ICE), and the time of day of that close is not stated by Yahoo. The gap to the TradingView ICE close (daily session ending 22:00 UTC) is largest for JPY (142.783 vs 143.575, 0.55%) and EUR (1.117718 vs 1.1161, 0.15%). Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $23,861,479,466,917, 0.16% lower. This is a deviation from the FX rule pending a ruling ([FX](../FX/2024.md#2024q3)).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
