YoY: each bank's change is in its own currency, so exchange rates don't move it; the Total's is in US dollars.

Each USD figure is the local amount × the FX rate, rounded to the nearest dollar (half-to-even); the total is their sum. Click an issuer for the bank's basic facts, a USD figure for the exact quote it comes from, and a rate for the exchange-rate source (each issuer extract carries the original and archived links). FX rates are market closes on or just before each report's count date (each FX row says which), or the mean of that month's weekday closes for a monthly figure (BOE, SNB, BCB); see [FX](../FX/2023.md#2023q4).

## What's left out

Nothing: all thirteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 29 December, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of December, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of December, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of December, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 29 December 2023, the last Friday of the quarter**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end is used. The figure comes from the supplement of 5 January 2024 and is observed, not adjusted to 31 December.

**Hong Kong's count date is 29 December 2023, not 31 December**: 31 December 2023 was a Sunday and 30 December a Saturday; the HKMA publishes no daily figure for either date (the API returns "No result found"), so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last business day before the quarter-end date is used.

**Growth rates.** [2024Q1](2024Q1.md) also counts all thirteen issuers: its total ($24,371,734,915,939) is $807,092,830,993 or 3.205% below this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $132,603,838,914 (0.529%) higher than [2022Q4](2022Q4.md)'s ($25,046,223,908,018), and $1,246,464,490,374 (5.208%) higher than [2023Q3](2023Q3.md)'s ($23,932,363,256,558).

**YoY count dates.** Korea's YoY compares the end of December 2023 with the end of December 2022, both end-of-month stocks, under the [BOK count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,444,995 ₹ crore on 29 December) is the printed total and equals its printed components, and it is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2023 column (4,386,759) plus the printed 2023-24 "Financial Year so far" change (58,236) gives 4,444,995 ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2023.md#rbi-reserve-money-2023-12-29-wss-2024-01-05)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,076.6 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 5,076,552 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2023.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2023-12-29-published-2024-01-04)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,521.4 billion rubles on 1 January 2024) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2023.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01012024-retrieved-2026-11-02)). The two tables differ on required reserves (200.7 narrow vs 271.7 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 January 2024 (18,320.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Dec, 2023" row is the stock at the start of 1 January (likewise 17,911.9 on 1 December for "Nov, 2023"). The day before the start of 1 January, 31 December, was a Sunday with no Moscow Exchange trading (no rows from 30 December to 2 January), so the rate is the last close before it, Friday 29 December. The ruble rate (89.7081218 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is 0.40% above the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (89.351), the largest FX gap this quarter. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) traded on 29 December 2023 and closed at 90.36, 1.13% above TradingView, and the cross rate is 0.72% below that close ([rate](../FX/2023.md#fx-2023q4-rub)).
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 422,719 (R$ million) for 'dez-2023', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-dec2023-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-02)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. The SGS values page returned HTTP 502 throughout this work, so the adopted figure is the archived SGS JSON API response for the same series (see the extract). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,152,311,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-dec2023-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-02)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,895,862 (HK$ million) for 2023-12 as the adopted daily figure for 2023-12-29, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2023.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2023-12-29-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-02)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (658,748 AED million, Dec 2023, Statistical Bulletin February 2026) is the printed total and equals its four printed components; the CBUAE's "UAE Monetary Base (Monthly)" release for December 2024 prints 658.8 (AED billion) for Dec-23, 0.1 above the adopted 658,748 (AED million) rounded, because that release rounds each component and sums the rounded figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2023.md#cbuae-monetary-base-dec-2023-statistical-bulletin-february-2026-table-4-retrieved-2026-10-29)); a cross-check, never summed. The AED rate (3.6728 per USD) is 0.014% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (3.6723); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (6,202,611 NT$ millions, 2023M12, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals; the CBC's ERM-H End of Period Amount prints 62026 (NT$ hundred millions), the adopted figure rounded (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBC/2023.md#cbc-reserve-money-2023-12-31-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-05)); a cross-check, never summed. The TWD rate (30.6971 per USD) is 0.06% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (30.679); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (274,077 billion won, 202312, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total and equals its two printed components; the Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2023.md#bok-monetary-base-2023-12-31-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-11)); a cross-check, never summed. The rate is the 28 December close: 29 December 2023 was the Seoul foreign exchange market's year-end closing day and 31 December a Sunday, so under the holiday rule the last Seoul trading day's close is used (MarketWatch's 29 December close, 1294.53, is not used; without the substitution the BOK figure would be $211,719,311,256). The KRW rate (1289.84 per USD) is 0.046% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1289.25); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components (the Fed's 2,335.4 + 3,491.8 = 5,827.2 billion dollars); the BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, prints 18,521.4, equal to its components (18,320.7 + 200.7).
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 21 weekday closes, 1 to 29 December). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 December 2023 was a Sunday, so the CNY, JPY, RUB, AED and TWD rates are the last close before it, Friday 29 December; the EUR, INR and HKD rates are the 29 December close, the count date of the ECB statement, the RBI supplement and the HKMA figure. 25 and 26 December were local-market holidays for GBP and CHF, and 25 December for BRL, so under the holiday rule those days take 22 December's close in the month averages; the holiday calendars are cited, archived, in the [FX notes](../FX/2023.md#2023q4). The largest gap to the TradingView ICE cross-check is RUB (above); among the other rates it is CNY (7.1132 vs 7.0973, 0.22%), then KRW (1289.84 vs 1289.25, 0.05%); converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $25,193,370,246,548, 0.058% above the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
