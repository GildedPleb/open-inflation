## What's left out

Nothing: all fourteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 September, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Certificates of Deposit / Monetary Bill), end of September, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of September, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of September, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)). Australia's figure is the RBA's own money base (notes and coins held by the private sector, banks' deposits with the RBA and other RBA liabilities to the private non-bank sector), September average (inferred), used as published (see the [RBA README](../issuers/RBA/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 25 September 2020, not 30 September**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end, 25 September 2020, is used (30 September 2020 was a Wednesday). The figure comes from the supplement of 2 October 2020 and is observed and final, not adjusted to quarter end.

**The euro area's statement date is 25 September 2020, not 30 September**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last statement dated on or before the quarter end, as at 25 September 2020 (published 29 September), is used; the next one, published 7 October, is dated after the quarter end.

**Hong Kong's count date is 30 September 2020**: 30 September 2020 was a Wednesday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (National Day was Thursday 1 October and the day following the Chinese Mid-Autumn Festival was Friday 2 October), and the HKMA API returns a daily figure for that date, so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** This quarter's total is $1,335,279,229,114 or 5.928% above [2020Q2](2020Q2.md)'s ($22,524,880,685,150). [2020Q4](2020Q4.md) also counts all fourteen issuers: its total ($26,018,170,361,725) is $2,158,010,447,461 or 9.044% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q3](2021Q3.md) ($28,465,045,569,309), which is $4,604,885,655,045 or 19.299% above this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** This page has no year-ago quarter, so there is no YoY count-date comparison here. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q3](2021Q3.md), whose YoY count dates are noted there.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The Federal Reserve figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (3,189,358 ₹ crore on 25 September) is the printed total; its printed components sum to 3,189,359, 1 above it, a rounding difference. Within the same supplement, the 31 March 2020 column (3,029,707) plus the printed 2020-21 "Financial Year so far" change (159,652) gives 3,189,359, also 1 above from rounding. Consistency check against the next issue, read, not adopted: the supplement dated 9 October 2020 prints 3,214,527 for 2 October with a week change of 25,169, which implies 3,189,358 on 25 September, equal to the adopted figure ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2020.md#rbi-reserve-money-2020-09-25-wss-2020-10-02)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 4,442.1 billion, is the summed items (EUR 4,442,135 million) rounded to one decimal in billions ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2020.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2020-09-25-published-2020-09-29)).
- **United States.** The adopted figure (4,880.3, not seasonally adjusted monetary base, September average) is from the 22 February 2022 H.6 release, the last HTML release that still prints September 2020. The first print, in the 15 October 2020 release, was 4,880.4 (currency 2,027.6 + reserve balances 2,852.8 = 4,880.4); it is kept as a not-adopted extract and named as superseded, under the FED latest-vintage rule. The printed total in the adopted vintage equals its printed components (2,027.5 + 2,852.8 = 4,880.3) ([extract](../issuers/FED/2020.md#fed-h6-monetary-base-2020-09-release-2022-02-22)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (13,235.0 billion rubles on 1 October 2020) is a different definition, not a conflict. Currency issued on 1 October 2020 (12,849.6) is the same in the narrow table, which confirms the broad "Sep, 2020" row is the stock at the start of 1 October. The printed broad total 18,524.3 is 0.1 above its printed components (12,849.6 + 3,039.7 + 673.2 + 1,148.3 + 813.4 = 18,524.2), a rounding difference; the printed total is used. Wednesday 30 September 2020 was a Moscow Exchange trading day, so the rate is the prior close before the start of 1 October. The ruble rate (77.839545 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB 11.4625 × the 30 September USD/CNY close 6.7908, per the FX rule, exact product) is 0.246% above the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (77.6484; denominator: the TradingView ICE close), the largest cross-check gap this quarter. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 77.63 on 30 September 2020, and the cross rate is 0.270% above that close (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close) ([rate](../FX/2020.md#fx-2020q3-rub)).
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-September 2020 is 408,736,990 (R$ thousand). The SGS JSON shows no components. Cross-check, read, not extracted: Table 28 of the BCB's October 2026 monetary and credit statistics tables prints 408,737 (R$ million) for 'set-2020', unmarked, with currency in circulation 354,308 and bank reserves 54,429 (354,308 + 54,429 = 408,737); the adopted figure rests on the archived SGS JSON ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2020.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2020-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-20)).
- **China.** The adopted figure (315,643.28 亿元) is the printed total; its printed components sum to 315,643.29, 0.01 above, a rounding difference ([extract](../issuers/PBOC/2020.md#pboc-reserve-money-2020-09-file-2025110510251391352)).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (416,816 AED millions, Sep 2020, November 2022 bulletin Table 4) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. Cross-check, read, not adopted: the October 2022 bulletin prints the same 416,816 and the same components for Sep 2020 ([extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2020.md#cbuae-monetary-base-sep-2020-statistical-bulletin-november-2022-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-20)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (4,678,165 NT$ millions, 2020M09, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2020.md#cbc-reserve-money-2020-09-30-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-20)). Cross-check, read, not summed: the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 46,782 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2020.09, the adopted figure rounded. The TWD rate (28.9297 per USD) is 0.023% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (28.923; denominator: the TradingView ICE close); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (216,881 billion won, 202009, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total; its two printed components sum to 216,880.9, 0.1 below, a rounding difference. The Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2020.md#bok-monetary-base-2020-09-30-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-20)); a cross-check, never summed. The rate is the 29 September close: 30 September 2020 was not a Seoul trading day: the BOK's own daily Seoul close series has no 30 September 2020 close, so the last close before the count date is used (MarketWatch's 30 September close, 1,164.77, is not used). The KRW rate (1,168.84 per USD) is 0.056% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1,168.18; denominator: the TradingView ICE close) and 0.056% below the BOK's own Seoul close that day (1,169.5; denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,168.84); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and both gaps are flagged.
- **Australia.** The adopted figure (170.3 $ billion, September 2020, RBA table D3 `DMAMMB` "Money base", monthly average, inferred) is the RBA's printed total; D3 prints no components, so there is no sum to check ([extract](../issuers/RBA/2020.md#rba-d3-money-base-2020-09-retrieved-2026-11-20)). The five archived earlier D3 vintages that print September 2020 (the earliest published 26-Feb-2021) print the same figure, so no earlier print is superseded. The AUD rate (0.722632 USD per AUD, the mean of 22 weekday MarketWatch closes) is +0.026% from the TradingView ICE mean of the same weekdays (0.722442; denominator: the TradingView ICE mean).
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except China's (0.01 亿元), India's (1 ₹ crore), the CBR's (0.1 billion rubles) and the BOK's (0.1 billion won), each noted above. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 22 weekday closes, 1 to 30 September; 7 September, Brazil's Independence Day, takes 4 September's close in the BRL average). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 September 2020 was a Wednesday; the CNY, JPY, HKD, AED and TWD rates are that day's close; the KRW rate is the last close before it, Tuesday 29 September, because the Seoul market was closed on 30 September; the RUB rate is the prior close before the start of 1 October, also 30 September; and the EUR and INR rates are Friday 25 September. GBP and CHF needed no weekday substitution. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2020.md#2020q3). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check are (MarketWatch − TradingView) / TradingView ICE close, flagged here and in each [FX row](../FX/2020.md#2020q3) with its denominator, since there is no divergence limit: RUB +0.246%; KRW +0.056%; CHF +0.052%; AUD +0.026%; TWD +0.023%; INR −0.023%; GBP +0.021%; EUR +0.017%; BRL +0.012%; AED +0.011%; HKD +0.007%; JPY −0.003%; CNY +0.001%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $23,859,990,650,668, 0.001% below the adopted total. The BOK's official Seoul close on 29 September (1,169.5) is 0.056% above the adopted MarketWatch close (denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,168.84), also flagged. The adopted ruble cross is 0.270% above the Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB close that day (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close 77.63).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
