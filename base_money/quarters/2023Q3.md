## What's left out

Nothing: all twelve basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 29 September, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of September, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of September, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 29 September 2023, the last Friday of the quarter**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end is used. The figure comes from the supplement of 6 October 2023 and is observed, not adjusted to 30 September.

**Hong Kong's count date is 29 September 2023, not 30 September**: 30 September 2023 was a Saturday and a Hong Kong general holiday (the day following the Chinese Mid-Autumn Festival); the HKMA publishes no daily figure for it (the API returns "No result found"), so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last business day before the quarter-end date is used.

**Growth rates.** [2023Q4](2023Q4.md) also counts all twelve issuers: its total ($24,966,338,600,991) is $1,230,565,319,279 or 5.184% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $708,020,840,517 (2.897%) lower than [2023Q2](2023Q2.md)'s ($24,443,794,122,229).

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source prints a figure that disagrees with an adopted one; the India check below finds a small unexplained difference implied by the next supplement.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,395,058 ₹ crore on 29 September) is the printed total and equals its printed components, and it is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2023 column (4,386,759) plus the printed 2023-24 "Financial Year so far" change (8,299) gives 4,395,058. The next supplement (6 October 2023 figures, published 13 October) prints 4,389,988 with a week change of −5,318, which implies 4,395,306 for 29 September, 248 (₹ crore, 0.006%) above the adopted figure, almost all in currency in circulation; the year-on-year change in the supplement of 4 October 2024 implies 4,395,307. No supplement found prints a revised 29 September level, so the difference is recorded as unexplained, the printed figure is used unchanged and no revision is appended ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2023.md#rbi-reserve-money-2023-09-29-wss-2023-10-06)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,198.3 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 5,198,340 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2023.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2023-09-29-published-2023-10-04)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (18,550.4 billion rubles on 1 October 2023) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2023.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01102023-retrieved-2026-11-03)). The two tables differ on required reserves (200.7 narrow vs 271.7 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 October 2023 (18,349.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Sep, 2023" row is the stock at the start of 1 October (likewise 18,358.8 on 1 September for "Aug, 2023"). The day before the start of 1 October, 30 September, was a Saturday with no Moscow Exchange trading (no rows for 30 September and 1 October), so the rate is the last close before it, Friday 29 September. The ruble rate (98.5042972 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is **2.10% above** the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (96.4802), the largest FX gap this quarter. Part of it is that the two legs are from different days: the USD/CNY leg is the CNY row's 28 September close (7.3004), because 29 September was a mainland China holiday; with MarketWatch's 29 September USD/CNY close (7.2952) the cross rate would be 98.4341336, still 2.03% above TradingView. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) traded on 29 September 2023 and closed at 97.9675, 1.54% above TradingView, and the cross rate is 0.55% above that close ([rate](../FX/2023.md#fx-2023q3-rub)). Converted at the TradingView close instead, Russia's figure would be $256,870,321,579 rather than $251,592,069,630.
- **Brazil.** Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 statistics tables prints 404,467 (R$ million) for 'set-2023', the adopted SGS 1788 figure rounded, with currency in circulation 328,771 and bank reserves 75,696 (read, not extracted; cited in the [extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-sep2023-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-03)), with no '*', so the figure is not marked preliminary. The SGS values page answered this time, so it is the adopted source, and the SGS JSON API response for the same series, archived, prints the same figure (see the extract). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 1,099,706,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2023.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-sep2023-sgs-values-page-retrieved-2026-11-03)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,880,617 (HK$ million) for 2023-09 as the adopted daily figure for 2023-09-29, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2023.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2023-09-29-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-03)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (597,311 AED million, Sep 2023, Statistical Bulletin October 2025, the latest bulletin still served by the CBUAE that prints September 2023) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. The September 2023 bulletin first printed the same 597,311, marked "* Preliminary", with the same components, and the November 2025 bulletin (archived; its original now returns HTTP 404) prints the same figure unmarked; both are read cross-checks, never summed (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2023.md#cbuae-monetary-base-sep-2023-statistical-bulletin-october-2025-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-03)). From the December 2025 bulletin on, the table no longer prints September 2023.
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (6,104,216 NT$ millions, 2023M09, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals; the CBC's ERM-H End of Period Amount prints 61042 (NT$ hundred millions), the adopted figure rounded (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBC/2023.md#cbc-reserve-money-2023-09-30-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-05)); a cross-check, never summed. The TWD rate (32.2463 per USD) is 0.06% below the TradingView ICE close for the same day (32.227); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components (the Fed's 2,327.7 + 3,239.4 = 5,567.1 billion dollars); the BCB's SGS page shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, prints 18,550.4, equal to its components (18,349.7 + 200.7).
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 21 weekday closes, 1 to 29 September). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 September 2023 was a Saturday, so the JPY, RUB and AED rates are the last close before it, Friday 29 September; the CNY rate is the 28 September close, because 29 September was a mainland China public holiday (holiday rule); the EUR, INR and HKD rates are the 29 September close, the count date of the ECB statement, the RBI supplement and the HKMA figure. 7 September was a Brazilian national holiday, so under the holiday rule it takes 6 September's close in the BRL month average (4.946543; 4.946538 without the substitution); there was no September holiday for GBP or CHF. The holiday calendars are cited, archived, in the [FX notes](../FX/2023.md#2023q3). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check, flagged here since there is no divergence limit: RUB +2.10% (above); INR 83.18 vs 83.056, +0.149%; CHF 0.899529 vs 0.899064, +0.052%; JPY 149.37 vs 149.33, +0.027%; EUR 1.0573 vs 1.05709 and GBP 1.238467 vs 1.238224, both +0.020%; BRL +0.016%, AED 3.6730 vs 3.67253, +0.013%; HKD +0.008%; CNY 7.3004 vs 7.3005, −0.001%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $23,552,726,941,728, 0.027% above the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
