## What's left out

Nothing: all twelve basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 30 December, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of December, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of December, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 30 December 2022, the last Friday of the quarter**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end is used. The figure comes from the supplement of 6 January 2023 and is observed, not adjusted to 31 December.

**Hong Kong's count date is 30 December 2022, not 31 December**: 31 December 2022 was a Saturday; the HKMA publishes no daily figure for that date (the API returns "No result found"), so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last business day before the quarter-end date is used.

**Growth rates.** [2023Q1](2023Q1.md) also counts all twelve issuers: its total ($25,392,055,851,172) is $564,635,263,696 or 2.274% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter's total is $822,404,533,718 (3.426%) higher than [2022Q3](2022Q3.md)'s ($24,005,016,053,758). This quarter is the year-ago base for [2023Q4](2023Q4.md) ($24,966,338,600,991), which is $138,918,013,515 or 0.560% above this quarter's.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The UAE figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (4,195,145 ₹ crore on 30 December) is the printed total; its printed components sum to 4,195,144, 1 below, a rounding difference. It is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2022 column (4,068,887) plus the printed 2022-23 "Financial Year so far" change (126,258) gives 4,195,145 ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2022.md#rbi-reserve-money-2022-12-30-wss-2023-01-06)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,569.7 billion, matches the summed items (EUR 5,569,703 million) ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2022.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2022-12-30-published-2023-01-04)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (16,454.7 billion rubles on 1 January 2023) is a different definition, not a conflict ([extract](../issuers/CBR/2022.md#cbr-monetary-base-narrow-definition-01012023-retrieved-2026-11-06)). The two tables differ on required reserves (107.0 narrow vs 145.9 broad); the CBR's footnotes don't explain it, as in the other quarters. Currency issued on 1 January 2023 (16,347.7) is the same in both tables, which confirms the broad "Dec, 2022" row is the stock at the start of 1 January. The day before the start of 1 January, 31 December, was a Saturday with no Moscow Exchange trading (no rows from 31 December to 2 January), so the rate is the last close before it, Friday 30 December. The ruble rate (68.3759496 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB × USD/CNY, per the FX rule) is **5.69% below** the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (72.5), the largest FX gap this quarter and larger than in later quarters. Both legs are from the same day (the USD/CNY leg is the CNY row's 30 December close, 6.8983). The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 69.9 on 30 December 2022, 3.59% below TradingView, and the cross rate is 2.18% below that close ([rate](../FX/2022.md#fx-2022q4-rub)). Converted at the TradingView close instead, Russia's figure would be $336,946,206,897 rather than $357,268,895,612. Late-2022 ruble markets were fragmented (onshore vs offshore); the product's standing rule is the CNY/RUB MOEX cross, so the MarketWatch-leg cross is used and the gap is flagged.
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-December 2022 is 419,659,918 (R$ thousand). SGS 20306 "Reserve Money - Monetary Authority", BRL 956,834,000,000, is an alternative definition that no BCB primary source defines, never summed ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2022.md#bcb-sgs-20306-reserve-money---monetary-authority-dec2022-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-06)).
- **Hong Kong.** Table 7.2.1 of the HKMA's Monthly Statistical Bulletin (end-of-period figures) prints the same 1,916,172 (HK$ million) for 2022-12 as the adopted daily figure for 2022-12-30, with the same four components (cited in the [extract](../issuers/HKMA/2022.md#hkma-total-monetary-base-before-discount-window-2022-12-30-open-api-daily-figures-retrieved-2026-11-06)); a cross-check, never summed.
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (512,273 AED million, Dec 2022, Statistical Bulletin February 2026, the latest bulletin still served by the CBUAE that prints December 2022) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. The March 2023 bulletin first printed 518,773, with the same Currency Issued, Reserve Requirements and Monetary Bills but current accounts and overnight deposits of 134,447 rather than 127,947. The figure was revised down by AED 6,500 million (1.3%), all in that line, between the first print and the February 2026 bulletin; the CBUAE gives no reason, so the revision is recorded as unexplained, and the later, unmarked figure is used under the [CBUAE vintage rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions). The March 2023 bulletin is a read cross-check, never summed (cited in the [extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2022.md#cbuae-monetary-base-dec-2022-statistical-bulletin-february-2026-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-06)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (5,927,795 NT$ millions, 2022M12, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2022.md#cbc-reserve-money-2022-12-31-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-06)). The TWD rate (30.6413 per USD) is 0.22% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (30.574); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except India's (off by 1 crore in the components, noted above) and the Fed's (2,298.1 + 3,107.3 = 5,405.4). The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total. The CBR's narrow table, not adopted, also equals its components (16,347.7 + 107.0 = 16,454.7). From December 2022 the PBOC's Currency Issue includes e-CNY in circulation (note 4 of the 2022 balance-sheet file; end-December e-CNY 136.1 亿元); it is used as printed.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 22 weekday closes, 1 to 30 December). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 December 2022 was a Saturday, so the CNY, JPY, RUB, AED and TWD rates are the last close before it, Friday 30 December; the EUR, INR and HKD rates are the 30 December close, the count date of the ECB statement, the RBI supplement and the HKMA figure. 26 and 27 December were local-market holidays for GBP, and 26 December for CHF, so under the holiday rule those days take 23 December's close in the month averages; 25 December was a Sunday, so Brazil needed no weekday substitution. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2022.md#2022q4). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check, flagged here since there is no divergence limit: RUB −5.69% (above); TWD +0.220%; CHF +0.048%; INR +0.048%; JPY +0.037%; HKD −0.032%; GBP +0.031%; CNY +0.016%; AED +0.016%; BRL +0.008%; EUR +0.005%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $24,797,549,038,053, 0.120% below the adopted total.
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
