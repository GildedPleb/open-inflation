## What's left out

Nothing: all thirteen basket issuers are counted, so coverage is 100%. Brazil's figure is the BCB's restricted monetary base (SGS 1788), used as published; it leaves out banks' compulsory deposits on time and savings deposits and their remunerated voluntary deposits at the BCB, and the exigibilidades for funds not directed to rural and housing credit (see the [BCB README](../issuers/BCB/README.md#what-we-measure)). Hong Kong's figure is the HKMA's own monetary base (Certificates of Indebtedness, government notes and coins, the Aggregate Balance and Exchange Fund Bills and Notes), end of day 31 December, used as published (see the [HKMA README](../issuers/HKMA/README.md#what-we-measure)). The UAE's figure is the CBUAE's own monetary base (Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances, current accounts and overnight deposits at the CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit), end of December, used as published (see the [CBUAE README](../issuers/CBUAE/README.md#what-we-measure)). Taiwan's figure is the CBC's own reserve money (reserves held by other MFIs and currency held by the public), end of December, used as published (see the [CBC README](../issuers/CBC/README.md#what-we-measure)). Korea's figure is the BOK's own monetary base (currency in circulation and its liabilities to depository corporations), end of December, not seasonally adjusted, used as published (see the [BOK README](../issuers/BOK/README.md#what-we-measure)).

**India's count date is 25 December 2020, not 31 December**: before 15 December 2025 the RBI published reserve money for Fridays only, and it publishes a final quarter-end figure only for 31 March, its fiscal year-end, so under the [RBI count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last Friday on or before the quarter end, 25 December 2020, is used (31 December 2020 was a Thursday). 25 December was Christmas, but the RBI still printed a figure for that Friday. The figure comes from the supplement of 1 January 2021 and is observed and final, not adjusted to quarter end.

**The euro area's statement date is 25 December 2020, not 31 December**: the Eurosystem's weekly statement is dated Fridays, so under the [ECB note](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the last statement dated on or before the quarter end, as at 25 December 2020 (published 29 December), is used; the next one is after the quarter end. 25 December was Christmas and a TARGET closing day, so the EUR rate is the 24 December close.

**Hong Kong's count date is 31 December 2020**: 31 December 2020 was a Thursday and not a Hong Kong general holiday (Christmas Day was Friday 25 December and the first weekday after Christmas Day was Saturday 26 December), and the HKMA API returns a daily figure for that date, so under the [HKMA count-date rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions) the quarter-end date itself is used.

**Growth rates.** [2021Q1](2021Q1.md) also counts all thirteen issuers: its total ($26,773,106,574,868) is $909,676,982,143 or 3.517% above this quarter's; part of that is exchange rates, since each balance is converted at the market rate on its own count date. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q4](2021Q4.md) ($27,941,318,049,317), which is $2,077,888,456,592 or 8.034% above this quarter's.

**YoY count dates.** This page has no year-ago quarter, so there is no YoY count-date comparison here. This quarter is the year-ago base for [2021Q4](2021Q4.md), whose YoY count dates are noted there.

## Checks and possible conflicts

No source disagrees with an adopted figure. The Federal Reserve figure was revised down from its first print.

- **India.** The adopted figure (3,314,105 ₹ crore on 25 December) is the printed total and equals its printed components. It is consistent within the same supplement: the 31 March 2020 column (3,029,707) plus the printed 2020-21 "Financial Year so far" change (284,398) gives 3,314,105. Consistency check against the next issue, read, not adopted: the supplement dated 8 January 2021 prints 3,322,090 for 1 January with a week change of 7,985, which implies 3,314,105 on 25 December, equal to the adopted figure ([extract](../issuers/RBI/2020.md#rbi-reserve-money-2020-12-25-wss-2021-01-01)).
- **Euro area.** The ECB press release headline, EUR 5,004.4 billion, is the summed items (EUR 5,004,427 million) rounded to one decimal in billions ([extract](../issuers/ECB/2020.md#ecb-press-release-base-money-headline-statement-2020-12-25-published-2020-12-29)).
- **United States.** The adopted figure (5,206.5, not seasonally adjusted monetary base, December average) is from the 24 May 2022 H.6 release, the last HTML release that still prints December 2020. The first print, in the 14 January 2021 release, was 5,206.6 (currency 2,071.6 + reserve balances 3,134.9 = 5,206.5, 0.1 below that printed total); it is kept as a not-adopted extract and named as superseded, under the FED latest-vintage rule. The printed total in the adopted vintage (5,206.5) is 0.1 below its printed components (2,071.6 + 3,135.0 = 5,206.6), a rounding difference; the printed total is used ([extract](../issuers/FED/2020.md#fed-h6-monetary-base-2020-12-release-2022-05-24)).
- **Russia.** The CBR's narrow monetary base (13,826.3 billion rubles on 1 January 2021) is a different definition, not a conflict. Currency issued on 1 January 2021 (13,419.6) is the same in the narrow table, which confirms the broad "Dec, 2020" row is the stock at the start of 1 January. The printed broad total 18,472.4 equals its printed components (13,419.6 + 2,548.5 + 713.6 + 1,220.7 + 570 = 18,472.4). The Moscow Exchange had no session from 31 December 2020 to 3 January 2021, so the rate is the last close before the start of 1 January, Wednesday 30 December. The ruble rate (74.6787384 per USD, Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB 11.448 × the 30 December USD/CNY close 6.5233, per the FX rule, unrounded product) is 0.561% above the TradingView ICE USD/RUB close for the same day (74.262; denominator: the TradingView ICE close), the largest cross-check gap this quarter. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) closed at 74.415 on 30 December 2020, and the cross rate is 0.354% above that close (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close) ([rate](../FX/2020.md#fx-2020q4-rub)).
- **Brazil.** SGS 1788 end-of-December 2020 is 431,536,653 (R$ thousand). The SGS JSON shows no components. Cross-check, read, not extracted: Table 28 of the BCB's September 2026 monetary and credit statistics tables prints 431,537 (R$ million) for 'dez-2020', unmarked, with currency in circulation 370,441 and bank reserves 61,096 (370,441 + 61,096 = 431,537); the adopted figure rests on the archived SGS JSON ([extract](../issuers/BCB/2020.md#bcb-sgs-1788-monetary-base-end-of-period-balance-dec2020-sgs-json-api-retrieved-2026-11-19)).
- **China.** The adopted figure (330,428.14 亿元) is the printed total and equals its printed components ([extract](../issuers/PBOC/2020.md#pboc-reserve-money-2020-12-file-2025110510251391352)).
- **UAE.** The adopted figure (427,445 AED millions, Dec 2020, May 2024 bulletin Table 4) is the printed total and equals its four printed components. Cross-check, read, not adopted: the August 2023 bulletin prints the same 427,445 and the same components for Dec 2020 ([extract](../issuers/CBUAE/2020.md#cbuae-monetary-base-dec-2020-statistical-bulletin-may-2024-table-4-retrieved-2026-11-19)).
- **Taiwan.** The adopted figure (4,836,231 NT$ millions, 2020M12, EF12M01en) is the printed total and equals its two printed subtotals ([extract](../issuers/CBC/2020.md#cbc-reserve-money-2020-12-31-ef12m01en-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)). Cross-check, read, not summed: the CBC's English Excel table ERM-H prints 48,362 (NT$ hundred millions) for 2020.12, the adopted figure rounded. The TWD rate (28.0902 per USD) is 0.054% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (28.075; denominator: the TradingView ICE close); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule.
- **Korea.** The adopted figure (221,503.7 billion won, 202012, ECOS 102Y001 `ABA2`, end of period, not seasonally adjusted) is the printed total; its two printed components sum to 221,503.8, 0.1 above, a rounding difference. The Korean-language query of the same table prints the same figures (cited in the [extract](../issuers/BOK/2020.md#bok-monetary-base-2020-12-31-ecos-102y001-end-of-month-retrieved-2026-11-19)); a cross-check, never summed. The rate is the 30 December close: the BOK's own daily Seoul close series has no 31 December 2020 close, so the last close before the count date is used (MarketWatch's 31 December close, 1,085.72, is not used). The KRW rate (1,088.45 per USD) is 0.076% above the TradingView ICE close for the same day (1,087.62; denominator: the TradingView ICE close) and 0.198% above the BOK's own Seoul close that day (1,086.3; denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,088.45); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and both gaps are flagged.
- **Rounding in printed totals.** Every printed total used equals its components except the Federal Reserve's (0.1 billion dollars in the adopted vintage and in the first print) and the BOK's (0.1 billion won below its components), each noted above. The BCB's SGS JSON shows no components; the ECB and BoE figures are exact sums with no printed total.
- **FX: MarketWatch daily closes (FX rule amendment 2026-10-28).** Yahoo Finance serves hourly bars only for the last 730 days, so every rate here is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, one by-date link per rate, archived on the Wayback Machine (month averages: the mean of 23 weekday closes, 1 to 31 December). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 December 2020 was a Thursday; the CNY, HKD, AED and TWD rates are that day's close; the JPY and KRW rates are the last close before it, Wednesday 30 December, because the Tokyo and Seoul markets did not trade on 31 December; the RUB rate is the last close before the start of 1 January, also 30 December; and the EUR and INR rates are Thursday 24 December, the last close before their 25 December (Christmas) dates. In the month averages, 25 and 28 December were bank holidays in England and Wales and take 24 December's GBP close, and 25 December was a Swiss and a Brazilian holiday and takes 24 December's CHF and BRL closes. The holiday calendars are cited in the [FX notes](../FX/2020.md#2020q4). Gaps to the TradingView ICE cross-check are (MarketWatch − TradingView) / TradingView ICE close, flagged here and in each [FX row](../FX/2020.md#2020q4) with its denominator, since there is no divergence limit: RUB +0.561%; CNY +0.118%; KRW +0.076%; JPY +0.065%; TWD +0.054%; CHF +0.044%; INR +0.041%; EUR +0.028%; GBP +0.025%; AED +0.011%; BRL +0.010%; HKD +0.005%. Converted at the TradingView closes instead, the total would be $25,873,505,911,313, 0.039% above the adopted total. The BOK's official Seoul close on 30 December (1,086.3) is 0.198% below the adopted MarketWatch close (denominator: the MarketWatch close 1,088.45), also flagged. The adopted ruble cross is 0.354% above the Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB close that day (denominator: the MOEX USDRUB_TOM close 74.415).
- **Other aggregates.** None compared this quarter.
