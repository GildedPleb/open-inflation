These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 21 weekday closes in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). No rate date or averaged weekday fell on a local-market holiday, so no day was substituted. The ruble rate uses the USD/CNY close in the CNY row.

These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 20 weekday closes (3 to 28 June) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). The count date 30 June 2024 was a Sunday, so the issuers dated that day (PBOC, BOJ) and CBR, whose start-of-day 1 July stock has no close on the day before (MOEX prints no rows for 29 or 30 June), use the last close before it, Friday 28 June (`last close before`); the other dated issuers (ECB, RBI, HKMA) report for 28 June itself. No rate date or averaged weekday fell on a local-market holiday, so no day was substituted. The ruble rate uses the USD/CNY close in the CNY row; TradingView's USD/RUB is an over-the-counter quote (the Moscow Exchange suspended USD trading from 13 June 2024, announced 12 June: [Moscow Exchange notice of 12 June 2024](https://www.moex.com/n70157), [archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20260116192324/https://www.moex.com/n70157); the Bank of Russia's [press release](https://cbr.ru/eng/press/pr/?id=39832) ([archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20250323032343/https://cbr.ru/eng/press/pr/?id=39832)) adds that US dollars "will still be traded in the over-the-counter market"); the adopted cross rate is 0.55% below it, the largest gap this quarter.
