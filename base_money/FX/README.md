# Exchange rates (FX)

Market rates used to convert each issuer's figure to US dollars, per the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule): ICE Data Services closes via Yahoo Finance, and the Moscow Exchange for the ruble. FX is supporting data; the Yahoo rates need not be archived.

Dates older than 730 days, where Yahoo has no hourly bars, use the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon), linked by date and saved to the Wayback Machine, with the TradingView ICE close as the check. MarketWatch does not say when its close is taken; it is about 20:00–22:00 UTC (INR about midnight UTC), always on the right date. Against our 22:00 UTC rates it is off by 0.01–0.07% on a typical day, and moves a quarter's total by less than 0.08%. On a local market holiday we use the last trading day before it.
