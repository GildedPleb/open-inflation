# Exchange rates (FX)

Quarter-end exchange rates used to convert each issuer's figure to US dollars, per the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule).

- **Source of record:** the Federal Reserve's [Foreign Exchange Rates, H.10](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/) weekly release; the [H.10 historical pages](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/hist/) confirm it.
- **Russian ruble:** the Bank of Russia's official rate, from its [XML_daily feed](https://www.cbr.ru/scripts/XML_daily_eng.asp); the [official exchange rates page](https://www.cbr.ru/eng/currency_base/daily/) confirms it.
