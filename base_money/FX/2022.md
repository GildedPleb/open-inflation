These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 22 weekday closes (1 to 30 December) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). The count date 31 December 2022 was a Saturday, so the issuers dated that day (PBOC, BOJ, CBUAE, CBC) and CBR, whose start-of-day 1 January 2023 stock has no close on the day before (MOEX prints no rows for 31 December to 2 January: [28 December to 3 January](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2022-12-28&till=2023-01-03>)), use the last close before it (`last close before`), Friday 30 December. The ECB statement is as at 30 December, a TARGET business day, and RBI (Friday 30 December, the last reporting Friday) and HKMA (30 December, its last business day of the quarter) report for that day, so those rows use the same day's close. 30 December 2022 was a business day in every calendar used: not a currency-derivatives holiday in India ([BSE notice 20211215-26, Trading Holidays for the Calendar Year 2022 for Currency Derivatives Segments](<https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20211215-26>), archived [Wayback 20261109203515](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261109203515/https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20211215-26>); it lists no December weekday, and 25 December is a Sunday), not a Hong Kong general holiday ([GovHK 2022](<https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2022.htm>); 25, 26 and 27 December), a workday in China (the State Council's [2022 holiday notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261109203429](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261109203429/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), has no holiday after 1 to 7 October, and its [2023 notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2022-12/08/content_5730844.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261026051536](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261026051536/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2022-12/08/content_5730844.htm>), starts the New Year holiday on Saturday 31 December 2022) and not a national holiday in Japan ([Cabinet Office national holidays](<https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>); none in late December 2022); MOEX traded CNY/RUB that day. In the month averages the [holiday rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) replaces a holiday's close with the last local business day's close before it: 25 December 2022 was a Sunday, so Christmas Day itself is not a weekday; England and Wales observed Boxing Day on Monday 26 December and the Christmas substitute on Tuesday 27 December ([GOV.UK](<https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>)), and CHF observed Monday 26 December (St. Stephen's Day) as a holiday (SIX's own 2022 currency calendar PDF is no longer served, HTTP 404 on 2026-11-09 with no Wayback copy; SIX lists St. Stephen's Day, 26 December, as a CHF holiday in its [2023 calendar](<https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), archived [Wayback 20261102195215](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195215/https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), and the same day in 2024, but the 2022 listing itself is not evidenced here), so those weekdays take Friday 23 December's close and each GBP/CHF average still has 22 weekday closes (the TradingView cross-checks are averaged the same way). Brazil's Christmas was Sunday 25 December and ANBIMA lists no Monday substitute ([ANBIMA feriados nacionais 2022](<https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2022.asp>)); the BRL market printed a close on 26 December, so no BRL day is substituted. Without the GBP/CHF substitution the averages would be 1.217018 (GBP) and 0.932145 (CHF). The `usdaed` CSV prints each price with a leading `..` (here `"..3.6726"`); the rate is the number after it. The ruble rate uses the USD/CNY close in the CNY row; the adopted cross rate is 5.69% below TradingView, the largest gap this quarter. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) traded on 30 December 2022 (42,116 trades) and closed at 69.9 ([Moscow Exchange USD/RUB, 30 December 2022](https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/USD000UTSTOM.json?from=2022-12-30&till=2022-12-30)), 3.59% below TradingView; the cross rate is 2.18% below that close. The TWD rate date, Friday 30 December 2022, was not a Taiwan market holiday ([TWSE Holiday Schedule of 2022](<https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/en/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule?date=20220101&response=json>)), so the TWD row uses that day's close.
