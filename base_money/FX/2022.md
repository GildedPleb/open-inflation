These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 23 weekday closes (1 to 31 March) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). The quarter-end date, 31 March 2022, was a Thursday, so the issuers dated that day (PBOC, BOJ, RBI, HKMA, CBUAE, CBC) use Thursday 31 March, and CBR, whose start-of-day 1 April 2022 stock uses the prior close, also uses 31 March. The ECB statement is as at Friday 25 March (the last statement dated on or before the quarter end), so the EUR row uses the 25 March close. 31 March 2022 was a business day in every calendar used: not a Hong Kong general holiday ([GovHK 2022](<https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2022.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261106133615](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261106133615/https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2022.htm>); Ching Ming Festival and Good Friday fell in April), a workday in China (the State Council's [2022 holiday notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261109203429](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261109203429/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), gives the Qingming holiday as 3 to 5 April and has no 31 March holiday), not a national holiday in Japan ([Cabinet Office national holidays](<https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>), archived [Wayback 20261021083545](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261021083545/https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>); the only March 2022 holiday is 春分の日 on 21 March) and not a Taiwan market holiday ([TWSE Holiday Schedule of 2022](<https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/en/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule?date=20220101&response=json>), archived [Wayback 20261110153102](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261110153102/https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/en/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule?date=20220101&response=json>); the next holidays are 4 to 5 April), not a currency-derivatives holiday in India ([BSE notice 20211215-26, Trading Holidays for the Calendar Year 2022 for Currency Derivatives Segments](<https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20211215-26>), archived [Wayback 20261109203515](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261109203515/https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20211215-26>); it lists 1 March as Mahashivratri and 18 March as Holi, and 1 April as Annual Bank Closing, but no 31 March weekday), and MOEX traded CNY/RUB that day ([31 March 2022](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2022-03-31&till=2022-03-31>)). Friday 25 March 2022, the EUR rate date, was likewise a business day. In the month averages the [holiday rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) replaces a holiday's close with the last local business day's close before it: England and Wales observed no March 2022 bank holiday ([GOV.UK](<https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>), archived [Wayback 20261022230654](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261022230654/https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>)), so the GBP average has 23 weekday closes and no substitution (the TradingView cross-check is averaged the same way); there was no March holiday for CHF (SIX's own 2022 currency calendar PDF is no longer served, HTTP 404 on 2026-11-09 with no Wayback copy, so this is not evidenced from a SIX 2022 page; SIX's [2023 calendar](<https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), archived [Wayback 20261102195215](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195215/https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), and its [2024 calendar](<https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2024-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), archived [Wayback 20261029142340](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261029142340/https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2024-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), list no March holiday either, but the 2022 listing itself is not evidenced here). Brazil's Carnival was Monday 28 February and Tuesday 1 March ([ANBIMA feriados nacionais 2022](<https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2022.asp>), archived [Wayback 20261110153134](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261110153134/https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2022.asp>)), so 1 March takes Friday 25 February's close (5.1630; [MarketWatch 25 February](<https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/currency/usdbrl/downloaddatapartial?startdate=02/25/2022%2000:00:00&enddate=02/25/2022%2023:59:59&daterange=d30&frequency=p1d&csvdownload=true&downloadpartial=false&newdates=false>), archived [Wayback 20261110152912](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261110152912/https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/currency/usdbrl/downloaddatapartial?startdate=02/25/2022%2000:00:00&enddate=02/25/2022%2023:59:59&daterange=d30&frequency=p1d&csvdownload=true&downloadpartial=false&newdates=false>)); MarketWatch's 1 March bar is not flat (close 5.1603). The MarketWatch link for the AED row uses an unpadded date (`startdate=3/31/2022`, not `03/31/2022`): a Wayback snapshot of the zero-padded URL replayed HTTP 401, and the cited capture of the unpadded URL serves the same one-day CSV (close `..3.6730`). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). The quarter-end date, 30 June 2022, was a Thursday, so the issuers dated that day (PBOC, BOJ, HKMA, CBUAE, CBC) use Thursday 30 June, and CBR, whose start-of-day 1 July 2022 stock uses the prior close, also uses 30 June. The ECB statement is as at Friday 24 June (the last statement dated on or before the quarter end) and the RBI count date is Friday 24 June (the last Friday on or before the quarter end), so the EUR and INR rows use the 24 June close. 30 June 2022 was a business day in every calendar used: not a Hong Kong general holiday ([GovHK 2022](<https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2022.htm>); Tuen Ng 3 June, HKSAR Establishment Day 1 July), a workday in China (the State Council's [2022 holiday notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261109203429](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261109203429/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), gives the Dragon Boat Festival as 3 to 5 June and has no other June or early-July holiday), not a national holiday in Japan ([Cabinet Office national holidays](<https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>); none in June 2022) and not a Taiwan market holiday ([TWSE Holiday Schedule of 2022](<https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/en/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule?date=20220101&response=json>); Dragon Boat Festival 3 June), and MOEX traded CNY/RUB that day ([28 June to 4 July](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2022-06-28&till=2022-07-04>)). MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 22 weekday closes (1 to 30 December) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). The count date 31 December 2022 was a Saturday, so the issuers dated that day (PBOC, BOJ, CBUAE, CBC) and CBR, whose start-of-day 1 January 2023 stock has no close on the day before (MOEX prints no rows for 31 December to 2 January: [28 December to 3 January](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2022-12-28&till=2023-01-03>)), use the last close before it (`last close before`), Friday 30 December. The ECB statement is as at 30 December, a TARGET business day, and RBI (Friday 30 December, the last reporting Friday) and HKMA (30 December, its last business day of the quarter) report for that day, so those rows use the same day's close. 30 December 2022 was a business day in every calendar used: not a currency-derivatives holiday in India ([BSE notice 20211215-26, Trading Holidays for the Calendar Year 2022 for Currency Derivatives Segments](<https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20211215-26>), archived [Wayback 20261109203515](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261109203515/https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20211215-26>); it lists no December weekday, and 25 December is a Sunday), not a Hong Kong general holiday ([GovHK 2022](<https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2022.htm>); 25, 26 and 27 December), a workday in China (the State Council's [2022 holiday notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261109203429](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261109203429/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), has no holiday after 1 to 7 October, and its [2023 notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2022-12/08/content_5730844.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261026051536](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261026051536/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2022-12/08/content_5730844.htm>), starts the New Year holiday on Saturday 31 December 2022) and not a national holiday in Japan ([Cabinet Office national holidays](<https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>); none in late December 2022); MOEX traded CNY/RUB that day. In the month averages the [holiday rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) replaces a holiday's close with the last local business day's close before it: 25 December 2022 was a Sunday, so Christmas Day itself is not a weekday; England and Wales observed Boxing Day on Monday 26 December and the Christmas substitute on Tuesday 27 December ([GOV.UK](<https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>)), and CHF observed Monday 26 December (St. Stephen's Day) as a holiday (SIX's own 2022 currency calendar PDF is no longer served, HTTP 404 on 2026-11-09 with no Wayback copy; SIX lists St. Stephen's Day, 26 December, as a CHF holiday in its [2023 calendar](<https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), archived [Wayback 20261102195215](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195215/https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>), and the same day in 2024, but the 2022 listing itself is not evidenced here), so those weekdays take Friday 23 December's close and each GBP/CHF average still has 22 weekday closes (the TradingView cross-checks are averaged the same way).
