## 2020Q3
These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 22 weekday closes (1 to 30 September) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 September 2020 was a Wednesday.
