These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 21 weekday closes (1 to 29 December) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC; INR about 00:00 UTC the next day). The count date 31 December 2023 was a Sunday, so the issuers dated that day (PBOC, BOJ, CBUAE) and CBR, whose start-of-day 1 January 2024 stock has no close on the day before (MOEX prints no rows for 30 December to 2 January: [27 December to 3 January](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2023-12-27&till=2024-01-03>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195219/https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2023-12-27&till=2024-01-03>))), use the last close before it (`last close before`), Friday 29 December. The ECB statement is as at 29 December, a TARGET business day ([ECB, T2](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/t2/html/index.en.html>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261029142356/https://www.ecb.europa.eu/paym/target/t2/html/index.en.html>))), and RBI (Friday 29 December, the last reporting Friday) and HKMA (29 December, its last business day of the quarter) report for that day, so those rows use the same day's close. 29 December 2023 was a business day in every calendar used: not a currency-derivatives holiday in India ([BSE trading holidays 2023](<https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20221215-37>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195424/https://www.bseindia.com/markets/MarketInfo/DispNewNoticesCirculars.aspx?page=20221215-37>)); its only December holiday is 25 December), not a Hong Kong general holiday ([GovHK](<https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2023.htm>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195150/https://www.gov.hk/en/about/abouthk/holiday/2023.htm>)); 25 and 26 December), a workday in China ([State Council 2024 holiday notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202310/content_6911527.htm>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261029142349/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/202310/content_6911527.htm>)), "一、元旦：1月1日放假，与周末连休。") and not a national holiday in Japan ([Cabinet Office national holidays](<https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261029142403/https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>)); none in late December 2023); MOEX traded CNY/RUB that day. In the month averages the [holiday rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) replaces a holiday's close with the last local business day's close before it: 25 and 26 December were bank holidays in England and Wales ([GOV.UK](<https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261029142231/https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>))) and CHF holidays ([SIX 2023 CHF and EUR currency calendar](<https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195215/https://www.six-group.com/dam/download/securities-services/digital-assets/post-trade/2023-chf-and-eur-currency-calendar.pdf>))), and 25 December was a Brazilian national holiday ([ANBIMA feriados nacionais 2023](<https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2023.asp>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195215/https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2023.asp>))), so those days take Friday 22 December's close and each average still has 21 weekday closes (the TradingView cross-checks are averaged the same way). Each of those three calendars has no other December 2023 holiday. Without the substitution the averages would be 1.266324 (GBP), 0.864367 (CHF) and 4.895738 (BRL). For AED, the UAE's public-holiday list ([u.ae](<https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/public-holidays-and-religious-affairs/public-holidays>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20231206033805/https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/public-holidays-and-religious-affairs/public-holidays>)), "Updated on 22 Nov 2023") gives New Year Day (1 January) and National Day (2 and 3 December) by Gregorian date and its other holidays only by Hijri date; no Gregorian conversion is sourced here, so no AED day is substituted and the AED row uses the 29 December close. The `usdaed` CSV prints each price with a leading `..` (here `"..3.6728"`); the rate is the number after it. The ruble rate uses the USD/CNY close in the CNY row; the adopted cross rate is 0.40% above TradingView, the largest gap this quarter. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) traded on 29 December 2023 (23,540 trades) and closed at 90.36 ([Moscow Exchange USD/RUB, 29 December 2023](https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/USD000UTSTOM.json?from=2023-12-29&till=2023-12-29), [archived](https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195417/https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/USD000UTSTOM.json?from=2023-12-29&till=2023-12-29)), 1.13% above TradingView; the cross rate is 0.72% below that close.

In the month averages the [holiday rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) replaces a holiday's close with the last local business day's close before it: 8 June was a Brazilian national holiday ([ANBIMA feriados nacionais 2023](<https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2023.asp>) (archived [Wayback](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261102195215/https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2023.asp>)), "8/6/23 quinta-feira Corpus Christi"), so it takes Wednesday 7 June's close (4.9264 for MarketWatch's own 8 June close of 4.9240) and the BRL average still has 22 weekday closes (the TradingView cross-check is averaged the same way); without the substitution it would be 4.849277.
