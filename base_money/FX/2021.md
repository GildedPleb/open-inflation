## 2021Q1
These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 23 weekday closes (1 to 31 March) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 March 2021 was a Wednesday. The CBR's start-of-day 1 April 2021 stock uses the previous day's close (`prior close`), Wednesday 31 March, which was a Moscow Exchange trading day ([29 March to 2 April](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2021-03-29&till=2021-04-02>), archived [Wayback 20261119232505](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119232505/https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2021-03-29&till=2021-04-02>)). ## 2021Q4
These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 23 weekday closes (1 to 31 December) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 31 December 2021 was a Friday. Issuers dated that day whose local market was closed, and CBR, whose start-of-day 1 January 2022 stock has no close on the day before (MOEX prints no rows for 31 December 2021 to 2 January 2022: [28 December to 3 January](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2021-12-28&till=2022-01-03>), archived [Wayback 20261119205452](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205452/https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2021-12-28&till=2022-01-03>)), use the last close before it (`last close before`), Thursday 30 December: BOJ (31 December is a standing TSE holiday, [JPX](<https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/trading/domestic/01.html>), archived [Wayback 20261115063304](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261115063304/https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/equities/trading/domestic/01.html>); not a national holiday in the [Cabinet Office list](<https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>), archived [Wayback 20261021083545](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261021083545/https://www8.cao.go.jp/chosei/shukujitsu/syukujitsu.csv>)), CBUAE (Friday was still a UAE weekend until the Saturday–Sunday weekend took effect on 1 January 2022, [UAE Government](<https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/Sector-of-employment/working-in-uae-government-sector/working-hours-in-the-public-sector>), archived [Wayback 20261119205814](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205814/https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/Sector-of-employment/working-in-uae-government-sector/working-hours-in-the-public-sector>)), CBC (31 December 2021 is a TWSE "Adjusted Holiday (2021)", [Holiday Schedule of 2021](<https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/en/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule?date=20210101&response=json>), archived [Wayback 20261119205650](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205650/https://www.twse.com.tw/rwd/en/holidaySchedule/holidaySchedule?date=20210101&response=json>))) and BOK (Seoul year-end closing day: ECOS 731Y003 prints no close for 31 December, last close 30 December, [20 December 2021 to 5 January 2022](<https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticSearch/sample/json/en/1/10/731Y003/D/20211220/20220105/0000003>) (archived [Wayback 20261119215619](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119215619/https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticSearch/sample/json/en/1/10/731Y003/D/20211220/20220105/0000003>))). The ECB statement is as at 31 December, a TARGET business day ([long-term TARGET closing-day calendar](<https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2000/html/pr001214_4.en.html>), archived [Wayback 20261119205809](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205809/https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2000/html/pr001214_4.en.html>)), PBOC reports for a mainland workday (the State Council's [2022 holiday notice](<https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261119205441](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205441/https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/content/2021-10/25/content_5644835.htm>), starts the New Year holiday on 1 to 3 January 2022), and RBI and HKMA report for that Friday (31 December is not a 2021 Hong Kong general holiday, [gazette](<https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202005/15/P2020051300631.htm>), archived [Wayback 20261119205712](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205712/https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202005/15/P2020051300631.htm>); the HKMA API returns a figure), so those rows use the same day's close. In the month averages the [holiday rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) replaces a holiday's close with the last local business day's close before it: 25 and 26 December 2021 were a Saturday and a Sunday, so Christmas Day and St. Stephen's / Boxing Day themselves are not weekdays; England and Wales observed Christmas on Monday 27 December and Boxing Day on Tuesday 28 December ([GOV.UK](<https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>), archived [Wayback 20261116142853](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261116142853/https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>)), so those weekdays take Friday 24 December's close and the GBP average still has 23 weekday closes (the TradingView cross-check is averaged the same way). CHF had no weekday holiday in December 2021 (25–26 December fell on the weekend). Brazil's Christmas was Saturday 25 December and ANBIMA lists no weekday substitute ([ANBIMA feriados nacionais 2021](<https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2021.asp>), archived [Wayback 20261119205711](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205711/https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2021.asp>)); the BRL market printed a close on 31 December, so no BRL day is substituted. Without the GBP substitution the average would be 1.331778. The `usdaed` CSV prints each price with a leading `..` (here `"..3.6730"`); the rate is the number after it. The ruble rate uses the 30 December USD/CNY close (6.3729; [Tullett Prebon, via MarketWatch](<https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/currency/usdcny/downloaddatapartial?startdate=12/30/2021%2000:00:00&enddate=12/30/2021%2023:59:59&daterange=d30&frequency=p1d&csvdownload=true&downloadpartial=false&newdates=false>), archived [Wayback 20261119205029](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205029/https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/currency/usdcny/downloaddatapartial?startdate=12/30/2021%2000:00:00&enddate=12/30/2021%2023:59:59&daterange=d30&frequency=p1d&csvdownload=true&downloadpartial=false&newdates=false>); the CNY row itself is the 31 December close, because China was open that day) times the Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB close; the adopted cross rate is 0.0165% below TradingView. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) traded on 30 December 2021 (64,758 trades) and closed at 74.68 ([Moscow Exchange USD/RUB, 30 December 2021](https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/USD000UTSTOM.json?from=2021-12-30&till=2021-12-30), archived [Wayback 20261119205413](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205413/https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/USD000UTSTOM.json?from=2021-12-30&till=2021-12-30>)), 0.0091% below TradingView; the cross rate is 0.0074% below that close. The BOK's own Seoul close on 30 December was 1,188.8, 0.097% below the adopted MarketWatch close 1,189.96 (against MarketWatch's close); the MarketWatch close is used, per the FX rule, and the gap is flagged.
