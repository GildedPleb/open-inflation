## 2021Q3
These dates are older than Yahoo's 730-day hourly window, so under the [FX rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) (amendment 2026-10-28) each rate is the MarketWatch daily close (Tullett Prebon via FactSet) for its trading date, linked by date and archived; month averages are the mean of the month's 22 weekday closes (1 to 30 September) in one MarketWatch link, rounded to 6 decimals. MarketWatch does not state its close time (about 20:00–22:00 UTC). The count date 30 September 2021 was a Thursday. The CBR's start-of-day 1 October 2021 stock uses the last close before it (`last close before`), Thursday 30 September, which was a Moscow Exchange trading day ([27 September to 4 October](<https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2021-09-27&till=2021-10-04>), archived [Wayback 20261119221107](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119221107/https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/CNYRUB_TOM.json?from=2021-09-27&till=2021-10-04>)). ## 2021Q4

In the month averages the [holiday rule](../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#fx-rule) replaces a holiday's close with the last local business day's close before it: 25 and 26 December 2021 were a Saturday and a Sunday, so Christmas Day and St. Stephen's / Boxing Day themselves are not weekdays; England and Wales observed Christmas on Monday 27 December and Boxing Day on Tuesday 28 December ([GOV.UK](<https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>), archived [Wayback 20261116142853](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261116142853/https://www.gov.uk/bank-holidays.json>)), so those weekdays take Friday 24 December's close and the GBP average still has 23 weekday closes (the TradingView cross-check is averaged the same way). CHF had no weekday holiday in December 2021 (25–26 December fell on the weekend). Brazil's Christmas was Saturday 25 December and ANBIMA lists no weekday substitute ([ANBIMA feriados nacionais 2021](<https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2021.asp>), archived [Wayback 20261119205711](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205711/https://www.anbima.com.br/feriados/fer_nacionais/2021.asp>)); the BRL market printed a close on 31 December, so no BRL day is substituted. Without the GBP substitution the average would be 1.331778. The `usdaed` CSV prints each price with a leading `..` (here `"..3.6730"`); the rate is the number after it. The ruble rate uses the 30 December USD/CNY close (6.3729; [Tullett Prebon, via MarketWatch](<https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/currency/usdcny/downloaddatapartial?startdate=12/30/2021%2000:00:00&enddate=12/30/2021%2023:59:59&daterange=d30&frequency=p1d&csvdownload=true&downloadpartial=false&newdates=false>), archived [Wayback 20261119205029](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205029/https://www.marketwatch.com/investing/currency/usdcny/downloaddatapartial?startdate=12/30/2021%2000:00:00&enddate=12/30/2021%2023:59:59&daterange=d30&frequency=p1d&csvdownload=true&downloadpartial=false&newdates=false>); the CNY row itself is the 31 December close, because China was open that day) times the Moscow Exchange CNY/RUB close; the adopted cross rate is 0.0165% below TradingView. The Moscow Exchange's own USD/RUB (`USDRUB_TOM`, board CETS) traded on 30 December 2021 (64,758 trades) and closed at 74.68 ([Moscow Exchange USD/RUB, 30 December 2021](https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/USD000UTSTOM.json?from=2021-12-30&till=2021-12-30), archived [Wayback 20261119205413](<https://web.archive.org/web/20261119205413/https://iss.moex.com/iss/history/engines/currency/markets/selt/boards/CETS/securities/USD000UTSTOM.json?from=2021-12-30&till=2021-12-30>)), 0.0091% below TradingView; the cross rate is 0.0074% below that close.
