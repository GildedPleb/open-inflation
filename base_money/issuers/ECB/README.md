# European Central Bank (Eurosystem) (ECB)

- **Official name:** European Central Bank; figures are from the Eurosystem's consolidated statement
- **Country or currency area:** Euro area (the European Union countries that have adopted the euro)
- **Currency:** euro, EUR
- **What it is:** The central bank of the European Union countries which have adopted the euro.
- **Official website:** [ecb.europa.eu](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/)
- **Data pages used here:** [Weekly financial statements](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/annual-reports-financial-statements/wfs/html/index.en.html) (the consolidated weekly financial statement of the Eurosystem and its press release)

## What we measure

Consolidated weekly financial statement of the Eurosystem, liabilities items 1 + 2.1 + 2.2; weekly stock at the Friday close. See [Per-issuer definitions](../../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions).
