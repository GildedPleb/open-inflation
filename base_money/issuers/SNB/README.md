# Swiss National Bank (SNB)

- **Official name:** Swiss National Bank (Schweizerische Nationalbank; Banque nationale suisse)
- **Country or currency area:** Switzerland
- **Currency:** Swiss franc, CHF
- **What it is:** Conducts Switzerland's monetary policy as an independent central bank.
- **Official website:** [snb.ch, English](https://www.snb.ch/en/)
- **Data pages used here:** [SNB data portal, cube snbmoba](https://data.snb.ch/en/topics/snb/cube/snbmoba)

## What we measure

snbmoba "Monetary base" / "Notenbankgeldmenge", item N2; monthly average (inferred). See [Per-issuer definitions](../../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions).
