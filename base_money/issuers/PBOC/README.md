# People's Bank of China (PBOC)

- **Official name:** People's Bank of China (中国人民银行)
- **Country or currency area:** China
- **Currency:** Chinese renminbi (yuan), CNY
- **What it is:** The central bank of the People's Republic of China (Article 2 of the Law of the People's Republic of China on the People's Bank of China).
- **Official website:** [pbc.gov.cn, English](http://www.pbc.gov.cn/en/3688006/index.html) · [pbc.gov.cn, Chinese](https://www.pbc.gov.cn/)
- **Data pages used here:** the Balance Sheet of Monetary Authority (货币当局资产负债表), published on pbc.gov.cn as a new file each month; each file has its own address, so the addresses are in the year files' extracts rather than here
