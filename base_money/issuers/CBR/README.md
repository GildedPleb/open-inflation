# Bank of Russia (CBR)

- **Official name:** The Central Bank of the Russian Federation (Bank of Russia; Центральный банк Российской Федерации, Банк России)
- **Country or currency area:** Russian Federation
- **Currency:** Russian ruble, RUB
- **What it is:** The central bank of the Russian Federation, which has the exclusive right to issue currency and to protect the ruble (Article 75 of the Constitution).
- **Official website:** [cbr.ru, English](https://www.cbr.ru/eng/)
- **Data pages used here:** [Monetary Base (Broad Definition), xlsx](https://www.cbr.ru/vfs/eng/statistics/ms/mb_bd_e.xlsx) · [Monetary Base, narrow definition](https://www.cbr.ru/eng/hd_base/mb_nd/mb_nd_month/)
