# Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE)

- **Official name:** Central Bank of the UAE
- **Country or currency area:** United Arab Emirates
- **Currency:** UAE dirham, AED
- **What it is:** the UAE's central bank. It keeps the UAE dirham at a fixed exchange rate against the US dollar, so most changes in its monetary base are matched by changes in its foreign reserves.
- **Official website:** [centralbank.ae, English](https://centralbank.ae/en/) (archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261021035300/https://centralbank.ae/en/); the live page answers automated requests with a Cloudflare challenge, HTTP 403)
- **Data pages used here:** the "UAE Monetary Base" table of the CBUAE's monthly Statistical Bulletin, a PDF per release (Table 4 "UAE Monetary Base (Monthly)" in the February 2026 bulletin, Table 3 "UAE Monetary Base" in the June and August 2026 bulletins); each figure's exact PDF and its archived copy are in the year files ([2023](2023.md), [2024](2024.md), [2025](2025.md), [2026](2026.md))

## What we measure

The CBUAE's own monetary base, the end-of-month stock for the quarter-end month, row "Monetary Base" (In Millions of AED) of the bulletin's "UAE Monetary Base" table, read from the latest retrievable bulletin that prints that month, used as published, not adjusted. It is the sum of four components the table prints: Currency Issued, banks' reserve balances with the CBUAE ("Reserve Requirements" in the February 2026 bulletin, "Reserve balances of banks with the CBUAE" from the June 2026 bulletin), Banks & OFCs Current Accounts & Overnight Deposits of Banks at CBUAE, and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposit. The bulletin defines it:

- "Monetary Base, defined as the sum of Currency Issued (Currency in Circulation outside Banks and Cash at Banks),Total Banks' Reserves & Current Accounts at the Central Bank and Monetary Bills & Islamic Certificates of Deposits held by Banks" and "Data are as at the end of period unless otherwise indicated." (notes page of the [Statistical Bulletin August 2026](https://centralbank.ae/media/njajo2nc/statistical-bulletin-august-2026.pdf) (archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261006075711/https://centralbank.ae/media/njajo2nc/statistical-bulletin-august-2026.pdf)))

The legal definition is in Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025, Art. 1, which repealed Decretal Federal Law No. 14 of 2018 (Art. 185):

- "Monetary Base: Includes the following: Issued Currency. Aggregate balances of current accounts and deposits of Licensed Financial Institutions with the Central Bank, including Reserve Requirements, in addition to any other funds deposited with the Central Bank for the purpose of clearing and settlement operations. Outstanding balance of securities and financial instruments issued by the Central Bank." ([Federal Decree-Law No. 6 of 2025, Art. 1](https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/federal-decree-law-no-6-2025-regarding-central-bank-regulation-financial-institutions-and) (archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20260320053706/https://rulebook.centralbank.ae/en/rulebook/federal-decree-law-no-6-2025-regarding-central-bank-regulation-financial-institutions-and)))

Item (2) of the 2025 law counts "current accounts and deposits" of Licensed Financial Institutions; the 2025 law adds "and deposits" to the 2018 wording. The series uses the bulletin's figure, not the legal definition. The CBUAE's page on the monetary system still gives the definition in the words of the repealed 2018 law:

- "Monetary Base (MB) – defined in Decretal Law No. 14 of 2018 as the sum of (1) currency issued (currency in circulation outside banks and cash at bank), (2) aggregate balances of current accounts of licensed financial institutions with the CBUAE, including reserve requirements, in addition to any other funds deposited with the CBUAE for the purpose of clearing and settlement operations, and (3) the outstanding balance of securities and financial instruments issued by the CBUAE;" ([How the Monetary System Works](https://centralbank.ae/en/our-operations/monetary-policy-and-domestic-markets/how-the-monetary-system-works/) (archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261029160610/https://centralbank.ae/en/our-operations/monetary-policy-and-domestic-markets/how-the-monetary-system-works/)))

**Cross-check.** The CBUAE's separate "UAE Monetary Base (Monthly)" release (End of Period, Figures in Billions of UAE Dirhams) prints each total as the sum of its own components rounded to AED 0.1 billion. In its February 2026 issue the "Banks & OFCs Current Accounts & Overnight Deposits of Banks at CBUAE" line is AED 0.1 billion above the bulletin's figure rounded for Jun, Jul, Aug, Oct, Nov and Dec 2025, which puts the release total AED 0.1 billion above the bulletin's for Jun, Aug, Oct, Nov and Dec 2025 (for Jul 2025 the totals still agree after rounding); the CBUAE gives no reason, so these differences are recorded as unexplained. For Jan 2026 the components agree and the total differs by AED 0.1 billion from rounding alone. Of the quarter-end months used, only Jun 2025 and Dec 2025 differ; the year files quote each release. It is read, never summed.

**Not used.** The CBUAE's HTML statistics pages could not be retrieved (Cloudflare challenge), so the per-release PDFs are used. The Statistical Bulletin March 2026 (`https://centralbank.ae/media/kvffokv3/statistical-bulletin-march-2026.pdf`) returned HTTP 404 with an empty body when retrieved on 2026-10-29, so 2025Q1 is read from the February 2026 bulletin. The How the Monetary System Works page (cited above) also answers automated requests with a Cloudflare challenge (HTTP 403); its Wayback copy is cited with it.

## What's in, what's out

- In: currency issued, banks' reserve balances, banks' and OFCs' current accounts and overnight deposits, and Monetary Bills and Islamic CDs held by banks.
- Out: Monetary Bills and Islamic CDs held outside banks (the total outstanding is not documented); foreign official deposits (inferred: not a listed component).
- Coin: the CBUAE issues coin itself.
