# Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan) (CBC)

- **Official name:** Central Bank of the Republic of China (Taiwan)
- **Country or currency area:** Taiwan
- **Currency:** New Taiwan dollar, TWD
- **What it is:** Taiwan's central bank. It publishes reserve money (also called high-powered money or base money) as the monetary liability of the Central Bank: reserves held by deposit money banks and Taiwan Post Co., plus currency held by the public.
- **Official website:** [cbc.gov.tw, English](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/en/)
- **Data pages used here:** Statistical Database series [EF12M01en](https://cpx.cbc.gov.tw/API/DataAPI/Get?FileName=EF12M01en) "3.Reserve Money-B.End of Month By Period" (archived: [Ghostarchive](https://ghostarchive.org/archive/2j0LR)); each figure's exact row is in the year files ([2023](2023.md), [2024](2024.md), [2025](2025.md), [2026](2026.md))

## What we measure

The CBC's own reserve money, the end-of-month stock for the quarter-end month, field "Reserve money" (Millions of N.T. dollars) of Statistical Database series EF12M01en "3.Reserve Money-B.End of Month By Period", used as published, not adjusted. It is the sum of two printed subtotals: Reserves held by other monetary financial institutions (Cash in vaults + Deposits with CBC) and Currency held by the public (Currency issued − Cash in vaults). The series title and units are from the API metadata:

- "3.Reserve Money-B.End of Month By Period" / units "Millions of N.T. dollars" ([EF12M01en](https://cpx.cbc.gov.tw/API/DataAPI/Get?FileName=EF12M01en) (archived: [Ghostarchive](https://ghostarchive.org/archive/2j0LR)))

The CBC's explanatory notes define reserve money as:

- "The reserve money comprises all reserves held by deposit money banks & Taiwan Post Co. and currency held by the public, both of which are the monetary liability of the Central Bank. It is also referred to as “high-powered money” or “base money”…" (CBC explanatory notes on monetary statistics, live original [expl.pdf](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/public/data/831410401671.pdf); no archived copy available at retrieval; the series title above is archived with the data API)

The IMF-style note in the CBC's Notes.pdf uses the same scope in shorter words ("Monetary Base comprises reserves with the Central Bank held by other depository corporations and currency held by the public"; live original [Notes.pdf](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/public/data/EBOOKXLS/Notes.pdf); archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261105222614/https://www.cbc.gov.tw/public/data/EBOOKXLS/Notes.pdf)).

**Cross-check.** The CBC's English Excel table ERM-H (End of Period Amount, NT$ hundred millions; live original [ERM-H.xls](https://www.cbc.gov.tw/public/data/Ebanking/ERM-H.xls); no archived copy available at retrieval) rounds each adopted millions figure correctly. It is read, never summed, and is not used as an extract URL.

**Not used.** The daily-average series EF11M01en and the CBC's press-release daily-average reserve money (a different convention).

## What's in, what's out

- In: reserves held by other monetary financial institutions (cash in vaults and deposits with the CBC), and currency held by the public.
- Out: CBC negotiable certificates of deposit issued to drain liquidity (inferred, not stated by the CBC: issuing them reduces reserve money); foreign official deposits (inferred: not a listed component).
- Coin: the CBC issues coin itself.

See [Where the banks' definitions differ](../../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#where-the-banks-definitions-differ).
