# Bank of England (BOE)

- **Official name:** Bank of England
- **Country or currency area:** United Kingdom
- **Currency:** pound sterling, GBP
- **What it is:** The UK's central bank.
- **Official website:** [bankofengland.co.uk](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/)
- **Data pages used here:** [Bank of England Database](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/boeapps/database/) (series LPMAVAA and LPMBL22)

Count date: the date the bank's figure counts, as the bank gives it. YYYY-MM means the figure is the bank's average for that month.

[Status](../../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#status-and-vintages): the bank's own label. `as published`: the bank gives no status label, so the figure is the vintage shown on its release or publication date, or on the retrieval date when the page shows none. Later changes are added as revisions, never overwritten.

## What we measure

No official monetary base is published; we construct it from Bank of England Database series LPMAVAA + LPMBL22; monthly average. See [Per-issuer definitions](../../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions).
