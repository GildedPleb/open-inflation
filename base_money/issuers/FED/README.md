# Federal Reserve (FED)

- **Official name:** Federal Reserve (H.6 is published by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System)
- **Country or currency area:** United States
- **Currency:** US dollar, USD
- **What it is:** The central bank of the United States.
- **Official website:** [federalreserve.gov](https://www.federalreserve.gov/)
- **Data pages used here:** [Money Stock Measures, H.6 (release page)](https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h6/)

## What we measure

H.6 "Money Stock Measures", Table 1, "Monetary base", not seasonally adjusted; monthly average. See [Per-issuer definitions](../../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#per-issuer-definitions).
