# Bank of Korea (BOK)

- **Official name:** Bank of Korea (한국은행)
- **Country or currency area:** Republic of Korea
- **Currency:** South Korean won, KRW
- **What it is:** Korea's central bank. It has the exclusive right to issue currency in Korea, banknotes and coins (Bank of Korea Act, Art. 47 and 53), and publishes the monetary base (본원통화): currency in circulation plus its liabilities to depository corporations (banks' reserve deposits and vault cash).
- **Official website:** [bok.or.kr, English](https://www.bok.or.kr/eng/main/main.do)
- **Data pages used here:** ECOS (Economic Statistics System) table 102Y001 "1.1.1.1.4. Components of Monetary Base(End of)", read through the ECOS Open API, one query URL per month (for example [September 2026](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticSearch/sample/json/en/1/10/102Y001/M/202609/202609), archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261111133425/https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticSearch/sample/json/en/1/10/102Y001/M/202609/202609)); each figure's exact row is in the year files ([2022](2022.md), [2023](2023.md), [2024](2024.md), [2025](2025.md), [2026](2026.md))

## What we measure

The BOK's own monetary base, the end-of-month stock for the quarter-end month, item `ABA2` "Monetary Base(End of)" (Bil.Won, billions of won) of ECOS table 102Y001 "1.1.1.1.4. Components of Monetary Base(End of)", not seasonally adjusted, used as published, not adjusted. The table prints two components, `ABA203` "Currency in circulation" and `ABA204` "Central bank liabilities to depository corporations"; the printed total is used even where it differs from their sum by 0.1 (source rounding). The table title, units and items are from the ECOS metadata:

- English: "1.1.1.1.4. Components of Monetary Base(End of) ", unit "Bil.Won", items "Monetary Base(End of)", "Currency in circulation", "Central bank liabilities to depository corporations" ([StatisticItemList 102Y001](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticItemList/sample/json/en/1/10/102Y001), archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261111134346/https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticItemList/sample/json/en/1/10/102Y001))
- Korean: "1.1.1.1.4. 본원통화 구성내역(말잔, 원계열)", that is, components of the monetary base, end of period (말잔), original series (원계열), not seasonally adjusted ([StatisticTableList 102Y001](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticTableList/sample/json/kr/1/3/102Y001), archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261111140518/https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticTableList/sample/json/kr/1/3/102Y001))

The BOK's statistical glossary (ECOS `StatisticWord`, entry 본원통화; [query](https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticWord/sample/json/kr/1/5/%EB%B3%B8%EC%9B%90%ED%86%B5%ED%99%94), archived: [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20261111135817/https://ecos.bok.or.kr/api/StatisticWord/sample/json/kr/1/5/%EB%B3%B8%EC%9B%90%ED%86%B5%ED%99%94)) defines it as:

- "따라서 본원통화의 크기는 개인들이 보유한 화폐와 일반 은행이 중앙은행에 예치한 지급준비예치금 및 일반 은행이 보유하고 있는 시재금의 합과 같아진다. 즉, 본원통화 = 민간보유 화폐 + 은행의 지급준비예치금 + 은행의 시재금 = 민간보유 화폐 + 은행의 지급준비금의 관계가 성립한다."

In English (our translation): the monetary base equals the currency held by individuals plus the reserve deposits banks hold at the central bank plus the vault cash banks hold; that is, monetary base = currency held by the private sector + banks' reserve deposits + banks' vault cash = currency held by the private sector + banks' reserves.

**Cross-check.** The Korean-language query of the same table and month (labels 본원통화(말잔, 원계열), 현금통화, 중앙은행 대 예금취급기관부채; unit 십억원) prints the same figures. It is read, never summed.

**Not used.** Table 102Y002 "Components of Monetary Base(Average)" (the same base as a monthly average, original series; a different convention from the end-of-period stock used for the other issuers), and tables 102Y003 and 102Y004 (end of period and monthly average, seasonally adjusted; never used here).

## What's in, what's out

- In: currency in circulation (currency held outside banks), and the BOK's liabilities to depository corporations (banks' reserve deposits at the BOK and their vault cash).
- Out: the BOK's Monetary Stabilization Bonds (통화안정증권) (inferred, not stated by the BOK: not a listed component); government deposits and foreign official deposits (inferred: not listed components).
- Coin: the BOK issues coin itself (Bank of Korea Act, Art. 53).

See [Where the banks' definitions differ](../../../docs/methodology/base_money.md#where-the-banks-definitions-differ).
