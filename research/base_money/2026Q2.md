- H.10 pages (7 currencies): the fetch tool truncates the archived page before the 2026 rows, so the rate rows are quoted from the live pages, fetched 17:29Z, with the same Release Date (Monday, October 19, 2026).

It prints 77.7539 with a decimal point, not the decimal comma "77,7539" the methodology describes. The XML_daily `ValCurs Date` was not read and is unknown here.
